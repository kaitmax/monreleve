WHAT AN AUDIT IS FOR, AND THE TWO WAYS IT CAN LIE
====================================================================

The prompt names one failure mode: A CRITERION RECORDED AS MET ON THE
STRENGTH OF A TEST THAT CANNOT FAIL, a guard that never ran, or a
sentence copied from an earlier report.

THIS SESSION FOUND BOTH THAT ONE AND ITS MIRROR, and the mirror is
the one worth carrying forward because it is cheaper to produce:

  THE AUDIT THAT OVER-CLAIMS   marks a criterion MET on evidence
                               that could not have failed.
  THE AUDIT THAT OVER-REPORTS  records a finding against something
                               that was never broken. A red result
                               FEELS like diligence, so it gets
                               written down faster than a green one.

Both happened tonight. Both are in the audit document under F4 and
F5, with what produced them.
