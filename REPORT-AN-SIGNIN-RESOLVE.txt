WHAT THE MATRIX SAYS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
- "200 every time" is explained by the answer arriving after the client lost its socket: the proxy
  wrote 200 into a connection that existed only on the host side (X1, I-rep).
- Build type is not the variable: X1 (debug) shows the empty form under the network event; E3, E6, I1
  (minified) reach MAIN under the same delay without one. Spontaneous failures were audit 2 of 7 delayed
  runs and debug 0 of 2 - too few to separate anything; A3's 2/2 vs 0/2 is not explained by this
  session's data and is not claimed to be. Its runs cannot be re-read.
- E1 had no connectivity capture (the buffer was not saved); its cause is INFERRED from the same shape
  (both pending requests failed together, the /me then DELIVERED).
- WHY the emulator's Wi-Fi drops: observed, not explained. One spontaneous drop at ~3 min after a boot
  (19:09:17); after the forced toggle in P1 a burst of spontaneous drops every 12-20 s
  (19:50:30, 19:50:42, 19:51:02), each "Regulatory domain changed to country: 00" -> DISCONNECTED ->
  reconnected ~4 s later, then quiet. The real-world equivalent is a Wi-Fi/cellular handover.
- The silent retry: Android's HttpURLConnection re-sends an idempotent GET on a new connection when the
  pooled one fails (X3: two /me arrivals for one fetch; the Wi-Fi-toggle P runs: two arrivals, the
  second succeeded over cellular). So a network change only becomes a transport failure when the
  new connection cannot be made in time - which is why a Wi-Fi toggle alone was not deterministic and
  the proof uses airplane mode.
- Build-type comparison asked for: timeouts and retries are identical (one UrlConnectionTransport,
  15 s connect / 15 s read, no app-level retry); BuildConfig differs only in API_BASE_URL (the same
  host:port here); the R8 keep set is unchanged and the instrumented minified build parsed every /me it
  received (view=true settings=true). No R8 cause, so no keep rule was added.

HOW A LATE, FAILED OR UNPARSED /me WAS MAPPED (99d24cdf, read):
- transport failure -> AuthedOutcome.NetworkError -> MeFetch.Unreachable -> Onboard(fresh) (empty);
- non-401 status -> AuthedOutcome.Ok(body), status dropped -> the error JSON read as a MeView with no
  canton -> Onboard(DEFAULT_STEPS, nothing prefilled) (empty);
- unparseable body -> parseMeView null -> onboardingPlanFor(null) -> Onboard(fresh) (empty);
- complete-but-strict-refused -> "defensive" Onboard(fresh) (empty);
- pending: a spinner, no timeout of its own (the transport's 15 s apply).
