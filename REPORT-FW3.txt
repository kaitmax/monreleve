INCREMENT 5 - denominator and validation                 commit d578fa1
Created: lib/calc-engine/denominator.ts, lib/calc-engine/validate.ts, and
their two test files.

THE FLOATING-POINT FINDING. Prorating the simplified basis naively loses a
whole day. 240 * 45/100 * 7/12 is mathematically exactly 63, but in IEEE 754
it evaluates to 63.000000000000007, and the rule rounds UP, so it becomes 64.
Scanning employmentRate 1 to 100 against the eleven period fractions a
mid-year start can produce found three such cases:

  rate 45, period 7/12   naive 64   correct 63
  rate 85, period 7/12   naive 120  correct 119
  rate 90, period 7/12   naive 127  correct 126

A one-day error in the denominator moves the declarable rate, which is the
number filed with the AFC. ceilProrated multiplies before dividing and
normalises to six decimals before rounding up; all three cases are regression
tests. The comment states that the normalisation is float hygiene and NOT a
regulatory rounding rule, so it is not mistaken for one at a site where SPEC
7.3 requires the rule to be named.

The normalisation factor is written 1e6 rather than 10 ** 6 because the
increment-2 threshold guard caught the bare 10 - it cannot tell the base of a
power of ten from the mission cap. Naming the constant beat exempting the
file, and the guard earning its keep on the first new engine module is worth
recording.

resolveDenominator mirrors and does not judge: a non-positive supplied total
passes through for validation to reject, and nothing divides before that
check. The exact method surfaces EXACT_METHOD_UNVERIFIED.

Validation returns every applicable error - proven by a case triggering four
at once - and never throws, proven by handing it an input that is NaN in every
numeric field. TOTAL_WORK_DAYS_NOT_POSITIVE deliberately suppresses
DAYS_EXCEED_TOTAL: comparing against a non-positive total is meaningless and
would send a form two contradictory messages about one field.
