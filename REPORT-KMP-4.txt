WHY THE ZONE IS A SEAM OF ITS OWN, and why R463's "an injected kotlinx-datetime
Clock" alone would change the bytes a tap writes. A java.time.Clock is TWO things:
an instant source and a zone. deviceClaimNow is OffsetDateTime.now(clock).format(
"yyyy-MM-dd'T'HH:mm:ss.SSSxxx"), so clientClaimedAt's OFFSET is the clock's zone's
offset at that instant - "+01:00" on the S9 today, "+02:00" in EntryRepositoryTest's
Paris clock. A kotlinx Clock is only the instant source. Without the zone the
moved repository could not reproduce one claim string, and EntryRepositoryTest's
"2027-06-18T12:00:00.000+02:00" golden could not pass unedited.

WHY NOT TimeZone.currentSystemDefault() INSIDE :shared, which would make the zone
seam disappear: today the zone is the one the clock captured when it was BUILT
(Clock.systemDefaultZone() in MainActivity.onCreate, in DevRecorderActivity, and
per tap in EveningActionReceiver). currentSystemDefault() reads it per CALL. The
two differ for a person who changes zone while the activity lives, and the hard
boundary is that what a tap writes does not change. So the zone is injected,
captured exactly where it is captured today.

THE LOGGER IS "NONE", AND THAT IS A FINDING: the canary's Log.e is the only log
call in the four files. Once the canary is an injected reporter, nothing in the
moved code logs. RE-VERIFIED AFTER THE MOVE: a grep for Log./println(/print(/
Logger/NSLog finds nothing in the four moved files and nothing anywhere in
:shared commonMain; the same pattern finds Log.e in :app's AndroidRecordSeams.kt
- the positive control that the grep can see a log call.

HOW :app PASSES THEM - THE CONSTRUCTION SITE. LocalEntryRepository is built
positionally as (dao, clock, ...) at 3 production sites (MainActivity,
DevRecorderActivity, EveningActionReceiver) and in 14 test files - about 95
construction lines, counted by grep on b942ed4. :app's construction site is ONE
function in AndroidRecordSeams.kt, named LocalEntryRepository, with today's
java.time signature; it forwards to the shared primary constructor with every
seam explicit: the adapted clock, the clock's zone, :app's canary reporter and
:app's UUID mint. All 17 files compiled UNEDITED, and the place a reader sees
what :app passes is that one function. Recorded under DEVIATIONS, because R467 expected to show the
seam arguments in each touched test file; with this, no test file is touched.

The rhythm repository's construction (two production sites, MainActivity and
