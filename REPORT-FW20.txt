THE MEASUREMENT THAT OPENED THE TASK
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Increment 0 began with /sources' first test failing for a reason that
had nothing to do with /sources: the page served a 404. Port 3000 was
held by `tizicorp-app`, a container from another of the operator's
projects, and playwright.config.ts hardcoded localhost:3000 with
reuseExistingServer.

Rather than describe the risk, it was measured. The full suite was run
against that container:

    91 of 220 tests passed.

THE MECHANISM IS NOT ABOUT PORTS. Absence assertions are satisfied by
absence. /sources 404s on a foreign server; a 404 page has no horizontal
overflow; so every R127 assertion holds - scrollWidth <= clientWidth is
true, and "every element inside the viewport at 320 dp" is true because
all of nothing is inside it. The assertions that pass hardest against
the wrong product are the ones written to prove a defect is absent.

A STATUS CHECK WOULD NOT HAVE CAUGHT IT. The foreign app answers
/api/health with 200 and valid JSON of its own. The obvious guard -
"did the endpoint respond" - would have passed and released all 91. That
is why R169 requires a field NAMING the product.

Proven live against that container, zero specs executed:

    R169: the e2e suite refused to run.
      expected  frontalier at http://localhost:3000
      found     a JSON body at .../api/health with no "service" field

IT FIRED A SECOND TIME, in increment 5, against a SECOND foreign
application. Port 3100 - the port this task moved to - answered
{"status":"ok","db":"ok","queue":"ok"}. Another project had taken it
mid-task. The run moved to 3200.

Two foreign applications, two ports, one task. The first collision could
be called bad luck; the second is what a shared machine does.

WHAT THIS SITE DOES NOT KNOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Three tasks had refused to publish something for want of a source, and
each refusal lived only in the repository. A reader looking for one of
those figures met an absence with no way to tell a decision from an
oversight. The section is on /sources, after the provisional readings
and before the fixtures - the page's order of decreasing certainty.

From PARK-1:
  "Ce qu'un employeur doit ou non vous remettre - Rien ici ne dit qu'un
   employeur doive vous remettre un document indiquant votre part de
   teletravail, ni depuis quand, ni sous quelle forme. Le guide consacre
   a ce document est ecrit au conditionnel pour cette raison."

From PARK-3:
  "Les dates et les formulaires de la declaration francaise - Aucune
   date limite, aucun numero de formulaire, aucune marche a suivre case
   par case. Une date fausse sur cette page couterait a un lecteur bien
   plus qu'une date absente."

From PARK-4:
  "Le cout compare de l'assurance maladie suisse et francaise - Ce site
   ne dispose d'aucune donnee de primes qu'il puisse citer. Sur un choix
   qui ne se reprend pas, un chiffre approximatif serait plus dangereux
   que pas de chiffre du tout."

NONE SAYS WHEN IT WILL ARRIVE. That is a promise, and the e2e test
asserts the absence of one directly. R165 applied: authored for a
stranger, not lifted from entries written for the operator.


THE FIGURE PARAGRAPH  (/a-propos)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
"Comment un chiffre arrive sur ce site - Une information reglementaire
 ne s'ecrit ici qu'au terme des memes trois etapes, sans exception."

  Une source primaire        Le texte de l'administration qui publie la
                             regle, jamais un article qui la rapporte.
  Une date de releve         Le jour ou cette source a ete lue. Une
                             regle sans date est une regle dont
                             personne ne sait si elle est encore vraie.

"Et si aucune source primaire ne peut etre trouvee, le chiffre n'est pas

a reader arrives with: "Personne n'a valide ces regles."
