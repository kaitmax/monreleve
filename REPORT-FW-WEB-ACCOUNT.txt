R899 - THE WEB SESSION (saved, not landed)
  WebSession is an additive migration. Its columns:
    id; userId; tokenHash (the SHA-256 hex of the cookie value);
    createdAt and expiresAt (from ONE server instant); revokedAt;
    revokedReason (the existing SessionRevocation enum).
  Its four CHECKs are asserted both ways, by name:
    WebSession_short_life (expiresAt > createdAt AND <= createdAt + 30 min);
    WebSession_revoked_pair; WebSession_revoked_after_created;
    WebSession_token_hash_shape.
  The life is stated as WEB_SESSION_TTL_MS = 30 min, and asserted equal to
  the cookie's Max-Age (1800) and to the CHECK interval read from pg_constraint.
  Sign-out revokes server-side. Revoke-all ends web sessions in the same
  transaction. Account deletion cascades.
  Disclosed as DISCLOSED_RECORDS.webSession. Exported as webSessions, without
  the hash.
  RED:
    red-r899-personal-data.log: 7 columns undisclosed.
    red-r899-web-session.log: 21 failed, "The table public.WebSession does
      not exist".
