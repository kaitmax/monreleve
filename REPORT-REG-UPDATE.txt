The passage (accord A, 1(d), verbatim above): the 10-day annual MISSION limit
"est ajustee proportionnellement et arrondie a l'unite superieure" for (i)
employees active less than a full calendar year AND (ii) part-time employees
over the whole year. 1(d) does NOT condition this on how the working days are
computed - it adjusts the 10-day limit itself, which is a quantity separate
from the denominator (working days).

The engine's behaviour (web/lib/calc-engine/denominator.ts, missionCapFor):
under the EXACT method it sets rateFactor = FULL_RATE (100 %) and so does NOT
prorate the cap by employmentRate. Only periodFraction reduces it. This is the
PROVISIONAL R19 reading: "the supplied total already embodies the working-time
rate, so prorating the cap by employmentRate again would count the reduction
twice." That reasoning is about the DENOMINATOR; 1(d) prorates the LIMIT
regardless.
