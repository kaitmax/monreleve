WHY FW11 DID NOT START
================================================================================

The FW10 prompt requires its report to end with this sentence, verbatim, and
it is not decoration:

  FW11 MUST NOT START UNTIL THE CHECKPOINT A HUMAN GATE IS RECORDED IN
  docs/regulatory-sources.md (R50).

THE GATE IS NOT RECORDED. Checked, rather than assumed:

  - docs/regulatory-sources.md still carries its banner, unchanged:
      "VERIFICATION PENDING - Checkpoint A"
      "Nothing in this file has been checked against a primary source yet."
    And the banner states its own removal condition: "This banner comes off
    only at the Checkpoint A human gate, when docs/regulatory-sources.md is
    dated and signed and fixtures/geneva-cases.json is published for the
    Kotlin port."

  - Every row's Verified column reads "pending". Zero of them are filled in.
    The file's own rule: "none may be ticked from an advisory source."

  - The engine agrees, out loud, on every test run:
      "VERIFICATION TRACE: 87 of 87 fixture cases are unverified (all of them
       - Checkpoint A has not run)."

  - docs/regulatory-sources.md is unmodified by this session. Its own header
    dates it to FW3 with an FW5 addition.

SO THE STOP IS NOT A JUDGEMENT CALL. FW11 is the calculator form: the first
surface that shows a user a number produced by the engine. R50 exists because
a provisional constant behind a form is a different thing from a provisional
constant in a test - the first one gets believed. Nothing in this chain, this
session, or my own convenience relaxes it, and I did not start FW11 or write
any part of it, hidden or otherwise.

WHAT WOULD UNBLOCK IT: docs/regulatory-sources.md dated, signed, and its
Verified column filled in from primary sources - admin.ch, the AFC, and
impots.gouv.fr - with URLs and fetch dates, and fixtures/geneva-cases.json
published. That is a human's work by construction; it is the one gate in this
plan that no session can do for you.

A NOTE ON R54, so it is not lost: FW11 was to be built HIDDEN. That
instruction is untouched and still applies whenever FW11 runs. Nothing was
built, so nothing was hidden or exposed.
