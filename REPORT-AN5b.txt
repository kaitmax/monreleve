INCREMENT 0 -- R279 (the exact binomial bound) and R280 (ODbL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R279. AN6a's scoring harness used the rule of three (3/n) at zero errors, which
      prints 150% at n=2 and was then capped to a still-dishonest 100%. R279
      replaces the zero-error branch with the EXACT one-sided binomial,
      1 - 0.05^(1/n) (from (1-p)^n = 0.05); Wilson stays for k > 0; the two meet
      with no discontinuity, and the exact form is always < 1 so no cap is needed.
      upper-bound.test.mjs pins it against independently-computed values (node
      --test, 5/5 green):

        n= 1  ->  95.0 %   (rule of three: 300 %)
        n= 2  ->  77.6 %   (rule of three: 150 %, the impossible one)
        n= 3  ->  63.2 %   (rule of three: 100 %)
        n=10  ->  25.9 %   (rule of three:  30 %)
        n=17  ->  16.2 %   (rule of three:  17.6 %)  -- the worked example's HIGH count
        n=20  ->  13.9 %   (rule of three:  15 %)    -- here they nearly agree

      The worked example (android/scripts/fixtures/) re-run: K1 and K2 now read
      16.2% (was 17.6%), K3 reads 77.6% (was the capped 100%). The rule of three
      stays in docs/field-protocol-AN6b.md as the human-facing explanation; the
      code uses the exact formula; docs/detection-model.md was updated to match
      (R149 -- a doc that names the code's method must not go stale).

R280. Distributing the APK distributes the GeoJSON inside it. Rather than argue
      whether that is "Public Use" of a Derivative Database, the product complies:
      a licence-and-attribution notice ships beside the data at
      android/app/src/main/assets/border/switzerland.json.LICENSE.txt (ODbL 1.0,
      "(c) OpenStreetMap contributors", the source relation, the share-alike
      obligation), so the licence travels with the file. The simplified polygon is
      thereby available under ODbL 1.0 with attribution at that path; recorded in
      docs/detection-model.md. The in-app attribution SCREEN stays a NEEDS (AN9+).

THE SHARED AGGREGATION (R281, AC2) -- the fixture, and the plant
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
fixtures/aggregation-cases.json is read by BOTH web/lib/dossier/aggregate.ts
(toTeleworkInput, UNCHANGED) and the new ch.monreleve.app.dossier.toTeleworkInput.
The step before the two engines -- turning rows into a TeleworkInput -- was the
one derivation nothing bound, which is where FW12's tile defect lived. Now a
one-digit change fails both builds (the plant above).

The seven cases, each a scenario chosen for a defect it can catch:
  full-days-simple           CH rows are not an engine input; HOME is telework.
  half-day-pair              an AM/PM pair sums to exactly one day.
  absences-of-each-reason    an absence of each reason reaches no figure (D28),
                             asserted by dropping them and getting an identical
                             input, not merely by "excluded".
  missions-france-and-third  France and third-state counted separately.
  partial-year-midyear-start periodFraction 184/365, derived from the date.
  r120-rows-versus-portions  R120: one day, AM HOME + PM HOME. Counting ROWS
                             gives 2; summing PORTION-DAYS gives 1.0. The input is
                             1.0 -- a port that counts rows fails ONLY this case.
  exact-denominator          the supplied total flows through; a null with kind
                             EXACT errors (a database written around), not here.

WALKTHROUGH (driven on the emulator; where it stopped, and why)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On a real emulator (Pixel_10, headless), against the production container:
  - The app installs and launches.
  - The sign-in screen renders correctly (an5b-01-signin: "Connexion", the email
    field, "Recevoir le lien" in brand blue).
  - Typing an email and submitting reaches the container: the app POSTs
    /api/v1/auth/magic-link and moves to "Verifiez votre boite mail"
    (an5b-02-checkemail) -- so the app-to-production-container path works on the
    device.

STOPPED AT THE CALLBACK DRIVE, by the stop-loss. Driving PAST sign-in needs the
callback deep link fired at the emulator, bound to the email the app remembered
(login-CSRF, PendingSignInStore). The email had to be typed via `adb shell input
text`, and that proved unreliable TWICE: first it concatenated two input calls
("walk example.comwalk@example.com"), then it truncated "camille@test.ch" to
"camil". Both are `adb input` automation defects, NOT app defects -- the pending
email was read back from the app's own prefs each time and was exactly what the
tool had typed. Per the stop-loss (a step failing twice), the emulator drive of
onboarding -> record -> sync -> year was not forced further.

What covers the un-driven screens instead: the Paparazzi baselines (JVM, real
Compose via Layoutlib) render every one of them and were LOOKED AT (R184); the
AC2 live comparison drove the server round trip end to end; the controllers'
logic is unit-tested. The gap is specifically the on-DEVICE render of onboarding
and Aujourd'hui and the on-device sync-drain animation of the pending line -- a
NEEDS carried to a session with a stable input path.
