not the answer, so the census searched for every way a test can open a file:

  grep -rlE 'java\.io\.File|java\.nio\.file|walkTopDown|readText\(|readLines\(
             |readBytes\(|getResource|File\('
       --include=*.kt
       app/src/test app/src/androidTest shared/src/commonTest
       shared/src/androidUnitTest

known to walk two roots - and shared/.../CommonMainFenceTest.kt, known to walk

produced a clean, empty, entirely worthless census.
