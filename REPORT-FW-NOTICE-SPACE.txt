SUMMARY
====================================================================
The calculator's provisional notice rendered its bold reading name run into
the next sentence ("...des jours de missionVos jours..." and "...au decompte
exactVous utilisez..."). Cause: JSX drops the line break between the closing
</span> of the bold name and the {body} expression on the next line
(CalculatorForm.tsx:1491-1492 at base). Fix: one {' '} after </span>, the
same idiom the line already uses before the link. A new rendered-text test
covers every site of the pattern (R267). The two notice baselines were
regenerated, named first, and the produced set equals the expected set.
