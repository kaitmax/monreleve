R653 (prompt R637) - THE FRENCH CONTRIBUTION, parameter by parameter
====================================================================
All quoted verbatim, accented, in docs/regulatory-sources.md L7-L13 (from
sources-2026-10.md section 16 and the URSSAF pages); ASCII here.
  Formula / rate / allowance - CSS art. D380-2 I (in force since 01.01.2019,
    no end date): "Montant de la cotisation = 8 % x (A-0,25 x PASS)". URSSAF
    6a: "En 2026, le taux de cotisation d'assurance maladie est de 8 %"; no
    separate 2027 rate is published, the article applies until changed.
  Base and year - D380-2 I: A = "l'assiette des revenus definis au deuxieme
    alinea du IV de l'article L. 380-3-1 percus au cours de la derniere annee
    civile pour laquelle ces revenus sont connus"; L380-3-1 IV: "revenus
    definis selon les modalites fixees au IV de l'article 1417 du code general
    des impots"; CGI 1417 IV (in force 01.07.2026) includes, point c, income
    "exoneres par application d'une convention internationale relative aux
    doubles impositions". URSSAF 6a: revenues of "l'avant derniere annee".
  Rounding - URSSAF 6a: "Le montant de la base de calcul et la cotisation sont
    arrondis a l'euro le plus proche." (With whole-euro A no tie can occur.)
  Floor / ceiling - NONE written (report 6d, 8). Negative -> shown 0 EUR,
    flagged, worded as the site's reading: PARK-28 (reviewer).
  Household - URSSAF 6a: "Cette declaration est individuelle"; 6d: proches
    attached as beneficiaries; CLEISS (D8). One contribution per frontalier.
  Prorata - D380-2 II; not computed (full year), said on the page.
  PASS - 2026: 48 060 EUR (URSSAF, page of 01.01.2026); 2027: 48 900 EUR,
    ANNOUNCED (URSSAF, 20.10.2026: "Un arrete sera publie d'ici la fin de
    l'annee").
No parameter lacked a primary source, so no STOP was needed.

R657 (prompt R641) - THE CALCULATION
====================================================================
web/lib/lamal/compare.ts (pure; eslint fence: no calc-engine, React, Next,
node:). Inputs: year, A (whole euros), persons [{birthYear, withAccident}],
persons[0] = the frontalier. Outputs: the year's parameters, the persons with
age and class, the Swiss spread (lowest/median/highest household total across
the 13 insurers, CHF centimes + EUR at the ECB rate), the French contribution
(base, contribution, floored flag, allowance), and cheaper = france |
switzerland | depends (across the whole spread; a tie is depends). Missing
year -> YEAR_UNAVAILABLE, never another year's figures.
Tests (tests/unit/lamal/compare.test.ts, 26): allowance boundary (12 225 /
12 015 exactly -> 0, not floored; one euro below -> 0, floored), rounding
(base 6 -> 0, base 7 -> 1), no ceiling (1 000 000 -> 79 022), worked cases
(2027: 40 000 -> 2 222; 2026: 40 000 -> 2 239), household of one and of four,
age-class bounds (18/19, 25/26, year-dependent), accident per person, ECB
conversion (2 511,60 CHF -> 2 718 EUR), cheaper in each direction and on a tie,
years never mixed, refusals. RED first (missing module), then GREEN; one
expectation of mine was wrong and corrected (see the commit d6cc8a3).
Plus format.test.ts (integer formatting) and input.test.ts (field parsing).
