EACH ROW - WHAT HELD IT, WHAT HOLDS IT NOW, BACKGROUND-KILL SURVIVAL
====================================================================
Mechanism key: SAVEABLE = rememberSaveable (Compose; Bundle-backed, so it
survives a background kill, not only a config change); SEED = a Serializable/
primitive snapshot held by the composition root in rememberSaveable and passed
into the controller to re-seed it (the dependency-free equivalent of a
SavedStateHandle; see ADVISOR DECISIONS). EVERY row below survives a background
kill - no row relies on a plain ViewModel (which would not).

  NAVIGATION TAB (incr 2, f5bf7e7): was remember; now SAVEABLE
    (rememberShellTab = rememberSaveable; AppTab is a Serializable enum).
    Background kill: YES.
  DESTINATION (incr 2): was remember; now SAVEABLE + reconciled against the
    session (sessionReconciled: a saved MAIN restored while signed-out is forced
    to SIGN_IN; a restored RESOLVING is re-decided; a saved ONBOARDING is honoured
    while signed in, skipping the RESOLVING round-trip). Background kill: YES.
  CALENDAR MONTH + OPEN SHEET (incr 3, 7b00375): was remember controllers; now
    SEED (CalendarController's restored CalendarSnapshot, wired through
    MainGraph.build - R291 - and held by rememberMainControllers in
    rememberSaveable). The sheet REOPENS once the rows load, so its prefill is
    the recorded day's, re-derived not transcribed (R181). Background kill: YES.
  SHEET HALF-ENTERED PICK (kind/absence/note) + chooser expansion (incr 3): was
    remember SlotDraft; now SAVEABLE (rememberSaveable keyed by day+slot - same
    day restores the pick, a different day starts fresh). Background kill: YES.
  AUJOURD'HUI chooser expansion (incr 3): SAVEABLE keyed by slot (a recorded tap
    is already persisted in Room). Background kill: YES.
  PARAMETRES mid-edit fields + open confirmations (incr 4, 97132fd): was
    remember controller; now SEED (ParametresSnapshot). The open sign-out/delete
    confirmations REOPEN with their counts RE-DERIVED from the record; R290's
    before/after is RECOMPUTED (never a persisted figure, R181). This needed
    save() split into a COMPUTE half (decideSave, pure) and a COMMIT half
    (persist), so a restore recomputes without committing (re-running save()
    would persist when the rates happen to match - the advisor's trap). An
    in-flight SAVING is never restored - a restore is EDITING. Background kill: YES.
  PARAMETRES typed delete word / licences sub-screen / dropdowns (incr 4):
    SAVEABLE in-screen. Background kill: YES.
  ONBOARDING values entered (incr 5, 1e49798): was remember controller; now SEED
    (OnboardingSnapshot). An in-flight SUBMITTING restores as EDITING. BG kill: YES.
  SIGN-IN typed email (incr 5): was remember controller; now SEED
    (SignInSnapshot). A SUBMITTING restores as EDITING; stable screens
    (LINK_SENT, the error states) are kept. Background kill: YES.
  RELEVE expanded annex (incr 5): SAVEABLE in-screen; scroll already saveable.
    Background kill: YES.

ADVISOR DECISIONS
====================================================================
Q (architecture): seed + rememberSaveable, or convert controllers to ViewModels
  with SavedStateHandle?
A: seed + rememberSaveable; do NOT convert to ViewModels. Reasons recorded:
  (a) it keeps MainGraph.build and the controller-test idiom intact (R291 - the
  wiring stays the thing the JVM test builds); (b) controllers keep dying with
  the activity, so lifecycleScope stays correct and no lifecycle semantics change
  (a ViewModel would extend their lifetime across rotations, staling the
  construction-time settings snapshot); (c) the restore path runs on every
  rotation, not only rare process death, so it is self-exercising. Also: the
  lifecycle-viewmodel-savedstate artifact is present only as a BOM version
  CONSTRAINT (c) on the classpath, not a resolved dependency - and the chosen
  design needs neither it nor a new dependency (which would be ask-first). I did
  exactly this.
Restore-semantics rulings from the advisor, all applied:
  - never persist an in-flight phase (SUBMITTING/SAVING/RESOLVING coerced to
    EDITING / re-decided on restore);
  - prompts persist the OPEN flag and RE-DERIVE the content (counts from the
    record; R290's before/after recomputed, never a persisted pair; this forced
    the save() compute/commit split);
  - destination restored only when consistent with the session (sessionReconciled);
  - enums are Serializable, so tab/kind/absence need no custom Saver.
  - am kill (not force-stop) is the background-kill test; the two are distinct.
The advisor was REACHABLE and gave a full review at the architecture checkpoint;
no stalls this session.
