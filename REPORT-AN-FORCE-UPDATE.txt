  UNREACHABLE / STATUS / NOT_JSON / FOREIGN / MALFORMED. Strict: a non-200; a body that is not
  JSON; not an object; service missing, null, non-string or not exactly "monreleve"; the field
  ABSENT (absent is not null); a quoted "5", 5.0, 5.5, 0, -1, 1e3, true, an object, above the
