R1360 - THE MOVE
================================================================================
MainTabs(controllers, tab, onTabChange, forecastRoute, onForecastRouteChange, yearDownloadFailed, onRetryDownload,
onCompareEmployer, onEnableAlert, parametresTab, forecastBackHandler) emits, in this order, exactly what MainShell
emitted: the forecast/simulation state collectors, LaunchedEffect(forecastRoute) (AN-CALENDAR R475's refresh), the
other four collectors, the Scaffold with AppBottomBar and the `when (tab)`, then the forecast overlay (its system-back
slot first, then the Surface). It emits no wrapper node: the Scaffold and the overlay Surface are siblings as before,
so Android's comparison and disclosure overlays, composed after the MainTabs call, still stack above it.
WHAT STAYS OUT, AS PARAMETERS:
  - the STATE. `tab` (rememberShellTab) and `forecastRoute` (rememberSaveable) stay declared where they were in each
    shell, with their saved-state keys; MainTabs takes value + setter. Android writes both from outside the wiring
    (the notification extras, the comparison overlay's "voir mes journees").
  - the PARAMETRES TAB, a composable slot `(state, onOpenRhythm)`. Android passes its ParametresTab call unchanged
    (BuildConfig version, the :app overload's Material picker, the disclosure/evening/alert launchers); iOS passes
    ParametresScreen with its actions and the native picker.
  - the SYSTEM BACK of the forecast overlay, a slot. Android passes `{ onBack -> BackHandler(onBack = onBack) }` (no
    common BackHandler exists, iOS Ruling 3); iOS passes `{}`, as before the move (IosMainShell had none).
  - yearDownloadFailed / onRetryDownload, onCompareEmployer, onEnableAlert: Android's exact former expressions; iOS's
    named stubs (unchanged strings). These stubs stay stubs; building them is IOS-SHELL-2's.
THE MOVED BLOCK AGAINST MainTabs (diff-moved-tabs.txt, `diff` of MainShell's Scaffold..overlay at the base against
MainTabs's): every hunk is one of

  - `yearDownload.retry` -> `onRetryDownload`; `yearDownloadStatus is YearDownloadStatus.Failed` -> `yearDownloadFailed`;
    the comparison lambda -> `onCompareEmployer`; `{ onToggleThresholdAlert(true) }` -> `onEnableAlert`;

    ParametresTab call itself moved, verbatim with its comments, into Android's slot lambda);

     where the rest went; and inside the call, the moved code's own comments (AN-PULL R484, AN-RECON, AN-ALERT x2,
