R935. THE PAGE SIZE IS READ FROM THE BYTES
------------------------------------------
The reader: web/tests/support/pdf-pages.ts. No new dependency: it reads the
bytes as pageCount() does.
- pageBoxes(bytes): every page's effective box, in page-tree order.
  - It reads startxref, then the classic xref table (object -> offset), then
    the trailer's /Root, the catalog's /Pages, and the page tree in order.
  - "page 5" therefore means the fifth page a reader sees.
  - An inherited /MediaBox (ISO 32000-1 7.7.3.4) is honoured.
  - Leaves must equal the root's direct /Count.
  - In the test, the page-tree count must equal pageCount()'s, so the count
    the harness prints and the pages measured are the same pages.
- isA4 / a4Violations: one sentence per non-A4 page, naming the page, its
  object, its size in pt and in mm, and the expectation.

The stated values, asserted in tests/unit/export/pdf-pages.test.ts (R345):
- A4_MM = 210 x 297, portrait (ISO 216). Points are DERIVED: mm x 72 / 25.4
  (595.2756 x 841.8898 pt). No point value is typed.
- A4_TOLERANCE_MM = 1 per side. The reason, measured:
  - Playwright's own paper table asks for A4 as 8.27 x 11.7 in (read from
    node_modules/playwright-core 1.63.0, lib/coreBundle.js:36680), i.e.
    595.44 x 842.40 pt: +0.06 / +0.18 mm over ISO.
  - Chromium then writes 595.92 x 842.88 pt: +0.48 pt on each side over the
    request. For Letter (8.5 x 11 in, integral points) it wrote exactly
    612 x 792 (plant B), so the extra is a rounding of the non-integral A4
    request.
  - Total measured deviation: +0.23 mm wide, +0.35 mm tall. 1 mm is about
    three times the larger.
  - The test also asserts the tolerance CANNOT admit US Letter, US Legal,
    A5, A3 or B5, and DOES admit Playwright's 8.27 x 11.7 in. A4 turned
    landscape is refused.
- The tolerance was chosen after measuring this machine's render and BEFORE
  the image's render was read (the image figure came from gate:changed).
