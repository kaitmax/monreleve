SUMMARY
====================================================================
The header's nav now sits centre-right, 32 px apart; the Calculateurs
menu - which had one live child - renders as a link to that child
under the child's own label; and the footer is four equal columns
(two on a phone) carrying every published guide, the alert list, the
about page, and Sources moved to Le produit.

The one design problem the spec left open was how a CLIENT component
gets the guide labels without typing them (R181). Answer: a new
guideNavLinks() in lib/guides/registry.ts derives each link from the
article's own breadcrumbLabel, and PublicShell takes it as a REQUIRED
guideLinks prop. All 51 renders across 15 files pass it; forgetting it
is a type error, not a footer silently missing its guides.

Cropping a regenerated baseline found a defect no gate saw: in two
narrow footer columns, labels that wrapped to two lines filled their
44 px row exactly and ran into one another. Fixed with padding-block
on .footerLink (one-line rows unchanged), with its own test first.
