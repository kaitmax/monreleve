R872 - THE DIALOG'S WINDOW: TWO PROBES, MEASURED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both probes were throwaway classes, never committed and deleted afterwards. Both ran
as `:app:testDebugUnitTest --tests <class>`, which is neither a record nor a verify,
so they wrote only to app/build/reports/paparazzi. git status of
android/app/src/test/snapshots after each: nothing. Images, copied by name:
.param-evidence/probe/.

PROBE 1 (ParamProbeScreenshotTest)
  Log probe-r872.log: "BUILD SUCCESSFUL in 2m 40s", "STEP_EXIT=0 ABORTED=False".
  The first attempt is NO RESULT and is kept as probe-r872-noresult-1.log: cmd.exe did
  not find a bare `gradlew.bat`, because this environment does not search the current
  directory. The re-run named the full path.
  a. probeRateChangeDialog - :app's ParametresScreen with pendingRateChange set.
     The product's AlertDialog is drawn WHOLE and centred over the list, with the scrim
     dimming it. Paparazzi 1.3.5 draws a Compose Dialog's own window.
  b. probeDatePickerDialog - Material 3's DatePickerDialog, assembled in the test like
     MaterialStartDateDialog, over the list.
     The window is drawn, with its scrim, but NOT whole: the seventh weekday column
     and the confirm button's ParametresCopy.START_DATE_PICK are cut at the right.
  c. probeDatePickerContent - the DatePicker alone.
     Whole. Locale "fr": French strings and dates, the week starting on Sunday.
  d. probeListTall - the list at 4 000 px.
     It stopped at the ABOUT_HEADING heading. Hence 5 800 px, and device resolution
     (the 4 000 px image was 270 px wide).

PROBE 2 (ParamProbeWideScreenshotTest, Queue Ruling 7 point 3: artefact or width?)
  Log probe-r872-wide.log: "BUILD SUCCESSFUL in 57s", "STEP_EXIT=0 ABORTED=False".
  The same DatePickerDialog, over a plain background, at device resolution, at two
  widths: PIXEL_5 (1 080 px = 392.7 dp) and screenWidth 1 440 px (523.6 dp). The
  container edges were measured on pixel row y = 1800 with System.Drawing:
    probeDialogPixel5: white from x=100 to x=979 -> 880 px = 320.0 dp,
                       centre 539.5 (screen centre 540); top 389, bottom 1950
    probeDialogWide:   white from x=280 to x=1159 -> 880 px = 320.0 dp,
                       centre 719.5 (screen centre 720); top 389, bottom 1950
  RESULT: at both widths the window is centred and exactly 320 dp wide, and the content
  is cut at its right edge.
  - DatePickerDialog asks for a required 360 dp, so the clipping does NOT depend on the
    screen width. A wider screen gives the same 320 dp window and the same cut.
  - That reads as Paparazzi/Layoutlib sizing the dialog window, not as a width problem
    of the product on a 393 dp phone. This is reasoned from the measurement; the S9 was
    not consulted, since there is no device in this queue.
  - It does NOT clear phones narrower than 360 dp. There, Material 3's required 360 dp
    would not fit whatever the renderer.
  ALSO MEASURED: with locale "fr-rFR" the picker's resource strings were French
  (DatePickerTitle in French, transliterated here as "Selectionner une date") but its dates and weekday initials were
  English ("2 Mar 2026", S M T W T F). With "fr" (probe 1) both were French. Paparazzi's
  locale reaches resources and formatting differently, so a future PARK-PB2 baseline
  must check both by eye.

CONSEQUENCE
  No date baseline is recorded (Ruling 7, points 2 and 3). The clipped
  DatePickerDialog is a finding: see NOTICED BUT NOT TOUCHING and PARK-PB2. The
  state-driven AlertDialogs are recorded as composed, window and scrim included.

NOTICED BUT NOT TOUCHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
N1. Material 3's DatePickerDialog, as MaterialStartDateDialog composes it, is drawn
    clipped by Paparazzi 1.3.5: a 320 dp window holding 360 dp of content. Probe 2
    shows this does not depend on the screen width (above). Whether a phone shows it
    whole is the S9's to answer (the orchestrator parks it); on a phone narrower than
    360 dp it cannot fit.
N2. At 200 % on a PIXEL_5, SignOutStillPendingDialog cuts off the very text it exists
    to show (signOutPendingDialogLarge).
    - What is cut: the two dates and the sentence saying they are lost if the person
      signs out (R440).
    - Why, as read from the code:
      - the warning sits in AlertDialog's `text` slot inside a plain Column with no
        verticalScroll (DeleteDialog's has one);
      - the stacked confirm column takes its full height;
      - so the text slot is squeezed and its end clipped.
    - The phone was not consulted.
    - Recorded as-is and labelled. The fix (a scroll, or a different layout at large
      font) is a product change, so it is not this task's.
