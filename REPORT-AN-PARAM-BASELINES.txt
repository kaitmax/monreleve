WHAT THE SCREEN CAN SHOW. Five layers:

  - what ParametresOverlays composes over it: five edit sheets, two information

  - the start-date picker, composed by the START_DATE sheet.
  The rhythm editor (onOpenRhythm) is another screen with its own baselines, and the
  five legal rows open the browser. Neither is a sheet of this screen.

WHAT :app's TEST CODE CAN REACH, without a product seam (the HARD BOUNDARY):
  - PUBLIC: ParametresScreen (:app's 4-argument overload, and :shared's), LicencesScreen,
    RateRow, ParametresActions, EditTarget, the ParametresDatePicker typealias.
    ParametresState and its prompt types (RateChange, SignOutPrompt, DeletePrompt,
    DeleteOutcome) are public too.
  - :app-INTERNAL, so visible to :app's tests: isoToMillis / millisToIso.
  - NOT REACHABLE:
    - every sheet's content composable and every dialog composable is `internal` in
      :shared, a different module;
    - MaterialStartDateDialog is `private` in :app;

      ParametresScreen (`editing`, `showLearnMore`, `showExport`) or to the sheet
      (`pickerOpen`), and Paparazzi cannot tap.
  The dialogs, by contrast, are driven by ParametresState, so the public screen
  reaches them. R872's probe showed that their window is drawn.

WHAT WAS LOOKED AT
  a. probeRateChangeDialog. :app's ParametresScreen with pendingRateChange set.
     RESULT: the product's AlertDialog is drawn WHOLE and centred over the list, with
     the scrim dimming it. Paparazzi 1.3.5 draws a Compose Dialog's own window.
  b. probeDatePickerDialog. Material 3's DatePickerDialog, assembled in the test like
     MaterialStartDateDialog, over the list.
     RESULT: the window is drawn, with its scrim, but NOT whole at device size. The
     dialog starts about 36 dp from the left and runs off the right edge: the seventh
     weekday column and the confirm button's ParametresCopy.START_DATE_PICK are cut.
     DatePickerDialog uses usePlatformDefaultWidth = false and a required 360 dp
     width. The 393 dp PIXEL_5 should hold it whole and centred, so the clipping reads
     as Layoutlib's window placement, not the product's. This is REASONED, not
     measured: no device in this queue.
  c. probeDatePickerContent. The DatePicker alone, inside FrontierTheme.
     RESULT: whole, nothing clipped.
     With locale "fr" the week started on SUNDAY. A French device starts it on Monday,
     because "fr" with no region does not name a region's first day. So the baseline
     names fr-rFR. The product's users live in France; for fr-CH the layout is the
     same.
  d. probeListTall. The list at 4 000 px.
     RESULT: it stopped at the ABOUT_HEADING ("A propos") heading. Hence 5 800 px, and device
     resolution (the 4 000 px image was 270 px wide).
