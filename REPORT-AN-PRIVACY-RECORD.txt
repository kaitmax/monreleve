THE MECHANISM (web/lib/account/export.ts + web/tests/db/account-export.test.ts, the "R1251 (A14 Q5)" block, 7 tests).
- Owed fields for an export key K (table M = EXPORTED's inverse) are computed in three steps:
  - take every PERSONAL_DATA column of M;
  - remove COLUMNS_NOT_EXPORTED;
  - add DERIVED_FIELDS (the ...Label words, each naming its source column).
  A field carries its column's name under K, with no aliases.

  the "same record the page renders".

  through POST /api/v1/entries exactly as the app sends it, with detectedAt and clientClaimedAt (R204).
