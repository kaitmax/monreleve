SUMMARY
-------
The operator's ask was "update the web view to show the comparateur". Most of
it was already on main from FW-COMPARATEUR: the header menu, the footer, the
/sources page, the sitemap and the guides all link the comparator, and each was
confirmed in the rendered page (ALREADY PRESENT). Two things were new, and this
branch builds only those two:

     on the droit d'option (rows D1, D2, D3), one primary action to
     /calculateur-lamal-cmu. No figure, no currency, no verdict; a test scans
     the rendered card for them, and the same scan is shown to fire on the
     band (positive control).
  c. FAQ: a fifth question in "Vos questions", two sentences and a link to
     the comparator. FaqEntry gains an optional `link` (absent by default,
     asserted both ways) because an answer was a plain string with no link
     slot.

Two baselines regenerated, exactly the two named before the run:
page-accueil-desktop.png and page-accueil-mobile.png. Every other baseline is
untouched (130 passed on the comparison run).
