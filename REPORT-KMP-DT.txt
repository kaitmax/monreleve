THE BUMP DID NOT HAPPEN, AND THAT IS THE R550 RESULT, NOT AN UNFINISHED STEP.
kotlinx-datetime 0.7 replaced its own Instant and Clock with kotlin.time.Instant
and kotlin.time.Clock, and in the standard library this project is pinned to -
Kotlin 2.2.21 - those two are @ExperimentalTime at RequiresOptIn.Level.ERROR.
They become stable only in Kotlin 2.3.0. Both published 0.7.1 artifacts were
compiled against this repository (uncommitted probes, reverted):

  0.7.1 (regular)           34 "This declaration needs opt-in" errors in :shared
                            alone - the hard boundary's STOP condition.
  0.7.1-0.6.x-compat        no opt-in, :shared compiles (63 new deprecation
                            warnings), but :app's 5 java.time.Instant -> kotlinx
                            conversions fail: in that artifact the library's own
                            converter is internal. The only public bridges are a
                            hand-written mapping (R553 forbids it) or the standard
                            library's converter (experimental: the boundary forbids
                            it). And every line iOS-1a writes would be written
                            against a deprecated type - what this task exists to
                            prevent.

So no route keeps (1) no experimental opt-in, (2) the library's own converters
at the Android boundary and (3) only the datetime version changing. That is an
operator decision; it is asked as Q1 with three options and parked (PARK-Z1).
