R1140 - THE SCHEDULER, ITS MECHANISM AND ITS DRIFT
====================================================================
Files: android/app/src/main/kotlin/ch/monreleve/app/reminder/FallbackReminder.kt (pure: FALLBACK_REMINDER_TIME 19:00,
nextFallbackAt, bootFallbackAt, reachedFallbackTime, fallbackReminderDecision, fallbackWake, restoredEveningOptIn) and
FallbackReminderRuntime.kt (FallbackReminderScheduling ensure / replan / afterReboot / planNextAfterWake / cancel,
FallbackReminderWorker, maybeRemindAtFallback, FallbackReminderReplanReceiver); the manifest receiver; MainActivity
LaunchedEffect(Unit) ensure in the signed-in shell.
WHY THIS MECHANISM (R1140's three discriminators; none needs SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM):
  reboot - WorkManager keeps the request in its own database and re-registers it itself (seen below after an app
  update: the link came back under a new JobScheduler id); an alarm is wiped by a reboot AND by an app update.
  Doze - a JobScheduler job waits for a maintenance window or the screen coming on, i.e. until the reminder can be
  read; an inexact alarm is deferred the same way unless allow-while-idle, which wakes a phone nobody looks at.
  drift - each link is computed from the wall clock and the zone in force, so lateness never accumulates and 19:00
  stays 19:00 local across DST (tests at 2026-03-29 and 2026-10-25, Europe/Paris). Same library as
  SamplingWorker; no new dependency.
THE TIME GATE is "at or after 19:00 on the local day", never equality: a late wake still reminds; a wake pushed past
local midnight reads before 19:00 and shows nothing.
DRIFT:
  MEASURED (emulator API 36, Europe/Paris, screen on, standby bucket ACTIVE, deviceidle ACTIVE): planned at 18:41:22
  local with delay 1117 s -> target 19:00:00.251; the worker logged its decision at 19:01:02.711 = +62.5 s. The job's
  JobScheduler record carries a FLEXIBILITY constraint (API 34+ flexible scheduling), the likely source.
  READ, NOT MEASURED: in Doze the job waits for the next maintenance window (light doze: minutes; deep doze: windows
  spaced up to hours) or for the screen to come on, when pending jobs run; standby buckets (an app opened rarely:
  RARE / RESTRICTED) can defer further. 19:00 is a target; the copy names no time. The S9 measurement stays
  PARKAN-46/47's.
