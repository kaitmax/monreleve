====================================================================
CONTEXT
====================================================================
AN10 ONLY: the calendar - every day of the year, seen, and every
older day recorded, corrected or reviewed here.

Aujourd'hui records today and yesterday. Everything else happens in
the calendar, which makes it the screen where the RECORD becomes
visible: which days are filled, which are missing, which were late,
which were changed after the fact. For a document whose value is its
honesty, the calendar is where a person checks their own work before
anyone else does.

====================================================================
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
====================================================================
AC1. A month of DayCells with its legend; tapping a past day opens an
     edit sheet; saving goes through AN5's queue; the cell changes.
     Future days cannot be recorded. Weekends and future days carry no
     quality bar.
AC2. The record is legible: a missing past working day reads as
     missing, a day recorded more than seven days late is marked late
     from the SERVER's flag, and a day changed after it was first
     recorded says so, with when.
AC3. At 200 percent text the grid becomes a list above a measured
     threshold, and every row still says date, kind, quality and
     provenance in words - driven on the device and looked at.
