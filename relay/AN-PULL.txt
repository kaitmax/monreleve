WHY
====================================================================
SEED-RELEVE confirmed there is no server-to-phone pull. Someone who
changes phone or reinstalls sees their releve (the server's) but an
empty calendar and an empty Aujourd'hui, and would reasonably think
their record was lost.

R347 (new). ON SIGN-IN, THE APP DOWNLOADS THE ACCOUNT'S YEAR.
  - The current and the previous tax year, from GET /api/v1/entries,
    into the local store, tagged with the signed-in account (R303), with
    the server's values - provenance, detectedKind, firstRecordedAt,
    recordedAt - exactly as stored. It is not a new record: nothing is
    queued, nothing is sent back.
  - A DAY ALREADY WAITING TO SYNC ON THE PHONE IS KEPT: it is the newer
    choice and will sync. The download never overwrites a pending row.
  - Rows SET ASIDE for another account (R303) are never touched or shown.
  - Also on app start when a year the server has is empty locally - a
    cheap check, not a full download every time.
  - A failed download leaves the local store as it was and says so in
    words; the person can retry.

TESTS (test-first): a fresh install receives both years; a pending day
survives the download; another account's set-aside rows are untouched;
a failed download changes nothing; the download writes nothing to the
mutation queue (R340's shape - assert it).
