TASK FW13 - URL PARAMETERS AND SHARE
====================================================================
Acceptance criteria (restated; the report must prove each):
AC1. Round-trip property: for any valid TeleworkInput,
     decode(encode(input)) equals input - 10 000 generated cases,
     covering part-time, mid-year, exact method, all granularities,
     and both mission fields.
AC2. A param URL pre-fills the form and reproduces the result;
     unknown params are ignored; an invalid value falls back to the
     default AND surfaces a dismissible notice - never a blank page,
     never a 404. An empty "?" behaves exactly as the bare path.
AC3. noindex and a bare-path canonical are present exactly when
     params exist, and absent when they do not (R119: asserted by
     count). No personal data is representable in the schema - proven
     by a test over the schema's key set.

INCREMENT 1 - the schema and the codec
--------------------------------------------------------------------
- lib/params/schema.ts: the nine params from SPEC 9.4 exactly -
  canton, taux, tt, mfr, mtiers, methode, total, decompte, debut -
