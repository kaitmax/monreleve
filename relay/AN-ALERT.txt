====================================================================
1. WHEN
====================================================================
- The forecast is recomputed when the record or the rhythm changes, and
  once a day after the day's checks conclude.
- An alert is sent THE FIRST TIME the projection crosses a threshold -
  40 percent, or 49,9 percent - and NOT again while it stays crossed.
  If it falls back under and later crosses again, that is a new
  crossing. The last state per threshold is kept on the phone.
- NEVER TWO MON RELEVE NOTIFICATIONS ON THE SAME DAY. If the evening
  reminder is due that day, the alert's sentence joins that one
  notification - the record buttons stay (AN-NOTIF) - rather than
  arriving separately.
- OPT-IN, contextual: offered once on the forecast screen ("Me prevenir
  si mon rythme approche d'un seuil"), with its own switch in
  Parametres. Off until switched on. Android's notification permission
  only when it is switched on and Android requires it.

====================================================================
2. WHAT IT SAYS
====================================================================
- Private content: "Votre rythme prevu depasserait le seuil
  d'imposition" (or de securite sociale), and that it is a projection.

  that there is information about the year - no figure, no threshold

- Its own channel, "Seuils", so it can be silenced separately.
