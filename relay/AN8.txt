R279 (new, from AN6a). AT ZERO ERRORS THE SCORING HARNESS USES THE
    EXACT ONE-SIDED BINOMIAL BOUND, 1 - 0.05^(1/n), NOT THE RULE OF
    THREE. The rule of three is a large-n approximation: at n = 2 it
    gives 150 percent where the exact bound is 78 percent, and
    capping it at 100 makes the number possible without making it
    honest. The small-n rows are exactly where this test lives.
    Using the exact bound also removes the discontinuity between the
    zero-error formula and Wilson for k > 0. The rule of three stays
    in the field protocol as the explanation for people; the code
    uses the exact formula. Increment 0.
