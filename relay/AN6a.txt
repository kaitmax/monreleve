CONTEXT
====================================================================
AN6a ONLY: the detection instrument, built and proven against
SYNTHETIC tracks on the emulator. It is the half of Checkpoint G that
a session can do.

THE HALF IT CANNOT DO is measuring real GPS near a real suburban
border. An emulator replays whatever coordinates it is fed; it cannot
tell you how a phone behaves in Annemasse, in a Leman Express tunnel,
or on a Xiaomi that kills background work. That is AN6b - a person
carrying the phone across the border for weeks - and it is written up
in docs/field-protocol-AN6b.md for the operator's tester. This task's
job is to make AN6b possible, scoreable, and honest.

WHAT "PROVEN" MEANS HERE. Synthetic tracks prove the classifier does
what it was designed to do. They do not prove the design is right
for real signals. The report states that distinction in its first
paragraph, so nobody reads a green AN6a as a pass at Checkpoint G.

Read: SPEC 13; SPEC 22 open questions 7 (the accuracy threshold) and
9 (the polygon's source and licence); docs/field-protocol-AN6b.md.
