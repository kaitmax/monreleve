R287 (new). THE RELEVE IS THE SERVER'S DOCUMENT, AND IT SAYS WHAT IT
    DOES NOT CONTAIN. Aujourd'hui's year panel is computed ON THE
    DEVICE and includes unsynced days. The releve renders
    /api/v1/dossier - the SERVER's computation over what the server
    has received. The two can differ, legitimately, whenever something
    is pending. So the releve states it: "N jours enregistres sur cet
    appareil ne sont pas encore synchronises et ne figurent pas dans
    ce releve." Otherwise a person sees two different rates on two
    screens and trusts neither.

R288 (new). AN OFFLINE RELEVE IS A DATED COPY, NEVER A CURRENT ONE. The
    last dossier fetched is cached and shown offline - with its
    generation date and a line saying it may not reflect days recorded
    since. It is never presented as current.
