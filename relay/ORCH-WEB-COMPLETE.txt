====================================================================

  containers, databases, volumes and processes).

  worktree C:/wt/<task> with its own .session-lock.json, from a prompt
  relay/<TASK>.txt written by the orchestrator just before launch. The
  session pushes its BRANCH; the orchestrator verifies and merges.

  (QUEUE-STATE.md's floor bullet) still binds. Commit free is read in its
  own step before every gate; a selection starts only at >= its worst
  solo drop + 2 000 MB (gate:changed with e2e and visual 5 045;
  gate:clone 3 958; base gate:changed 3 954). A sampler abort or a red
  shaped like dead workers is NO RESULT, never a verdict.
- MERGE. Fast-forward only. If main moved, the branch is rebased by its
  session (or the orchestrator, in its own worktree) and gated again.
  Every merge to main runs gate:clone ALONE on the exact tip first.
- RECORDS. relay/WEB-QUEUE-STATE.md is this run's memory; QUEUE-STATE.md
  is the previous queue's closed record, and its BLOCKED 1-12, PARKED
  TASKS and PARKED PROMOTIONS still stand. Record commits ride with a
