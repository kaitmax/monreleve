TWO FAILURE MODES, and the second is the likely one:

  The audit that fails - a criterion is not met. That is useful and
  easy to write down.

  THE AUDIT THAT LIES - a criterion is recorded as met on the
  strength of a test that cannot fail, a guard that never ran, a
  claim nobody re-checked, or a sentence copied from an earlier
  report. This project has found, in eleven tasks: a suite passing 91
  tests against a different application; a regeneration reporting

  matched nothing; a job discarding every count it computed; a
  parity declaration asserting its own correctness; and an audit
  glob correct only by accident. Every one of those would have
  supplied a green line to an audit written from reports.

  SO: EVERY LINE IN THE AUDIT CITES EVIDENCE THAT WAS PRODUCED OR
  RE-RUN IN THIS SESSION, or it is marked UNVERIFIED. A report from
  an earlier task is a POINTER to evidence, never the evidence.
