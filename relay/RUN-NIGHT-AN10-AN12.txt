THE PARKING RULE IS THE POINT OF TONIGHT
====================================================================
R156. An open question does not stop the task. Take the conservative,
reversible option - the one that claims less, ships less, and can be
changed tomorrow without a migration, a correction to something
published, or an app release - write a PARKAN-<n> entry in
parking.md, and continue.

Each entry, appended, newest last:
  ## PARKAN-<n> - <one-line title>  [AN<task>, <date>]
  QUESTION:  what was unclear, in two sentences.
  TAKEN:     the conservative option, and why it is reversible.
  NOT TAKEN: the alternative, and what switching would cost.
  TOUCHES:   files, tests and baselines a switch would change.

THE BOUNDARY IS NOT NEGOTIABLE. Stop and write the report if the
decision would be PERMANENT (a Room schema version that ships, a URL,
a /v1 field), would PUBLISH A FALSE OR UNSOURCED CLAIM, changes a
gate or budget, adds a runtime dependency without a named reason,
touches either engine or the /v1 contract, reaches a real person or
third party, or leaves a gate red.

Two sentences to keep in mind while alone:
  - Shipping less is always available and always reversible.
  - The manual product never mentions what it lacks (R282), so a
    missing feature is omitted, not hinted at.

    next. Never read ahead.

C2. THE HANDOVER CHECK, recorded at the top of the next report's
    PRECONDITIONS:

      - git status is clean and local main equals origin/main;
      - pnpm gate:clone is GREEN on the pushed HEAD, with its claim

      - no orphaned emulator, adb server, container or port (R179);
      - the previous report carries nothing marked BLOCKING that R156

    commit, push, end.

    from disk; do not rely on memory of having written them. Check
    tasks/todo.md for each task's number.

C4. NO CROSS-TASK REFACTORING. A defect noticed in AN10's output while
    doing AN11 goes under NOTICED BUT NOT TOUCHING in AN11's report.

C5. CONTEXT DISCIPLINE, and it matters more tonight than usual. Three
    tasks with emulator work is a heavy session. Before each task,
    re-read CLAUDE.md and that task's prompt from disk. IF YOU CANNOT
