CONTEXT
====================================================================
AN4 ONLY: passwordless sign-in on the device, preceded by AN3's three
carried fixes.

THE CONSTRAINT THAT SHAPES THE WHOLE TASK. An Android App Link opens
the app only when the OS has VERIFIED the domain's assetlinks.json -
and monreleve.ch DOES NOT RESOLVE (FW29, Checkpoint C). So the real
flow - tap the link in the email, the app opens - CANNOT BE TESTED
END TO END in this task, and must not be claimed.

What CAN be tested, and is worth testing well: the app's HANDLING of
the callback, by firing the intent directly -
  adb shell am start -a android.intent.action.VIEW \
    -d "https://monreleve.ch/<callback path>?token=..."
That proves the intent filter matches, the parsing is right, the
exchange succeeds and every failure state renders. It does NOT prove
the OS verifies the link. Those are two different claims and the
report states both, separately.

Read: SPEC 10 (auth), SPEC 11.1 (onboarding), SPEC 20; docs/api-v1.md;
REPORT-FW22.txt; docs/android-skills.md - the android-intent-security
notes especially, which AN0 found harden the client side of R195.

INCREMENT 5 - refresh, replay, sign-out
====================================================================
- Refresh on expiry, once, transparently. A request that fails
  refresh lands the user on sign-in with an honest explanation.

  expiry (R210).

  authenticated call is refused - asserted, not assumed.

  ends the session (R210 turned against ourselves). Single-flight
  the refresh, and prove it with a test that fires two at once.
