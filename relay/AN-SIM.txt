R340 (new, and it governs this whole series). A PROJECTION IS NEVER A
    RECORD. A forecast or a simulation is computed in memory from the
    recorded days plus imagined ones, and:
      - it is NEVER written to the entries, the mutation queue, the
        server, the releve, the PDF or the annex - a test asserts that
        computing one leaves every store unchanged;
      - it is LABELLED "Projection" wherever it appears, drawn visibly
        differently from recorded figures (not by colour alone), with
        its assumptions in words;
      - its figures come from THE ENGINE, through the shared aggregation
        (R281) - never arithmetic in the UI (FW12's defect).
    The releve's value is that it records only what happened.

====================================================================
1. THE SCENARIOS - ONLY WHAT THE ENGINE MODELS
====================================================================
Each is a change to the imagined year, run through the engine and shown
against the current projection:
  - ONE MORE DAY AT HOME EACH WEEK - the person picks the weekday.
  - EXTRA TELEWORK DAYS THIS MONTH - a number.
  - A DIFFERENT EMPLOYMENT RATE - which changes the basis; REUSE R290's
    before-and-after calculation, never a second copy (R206).
  - MISSIONS - in France or in a third state, a number each; the engine
    applies the 10-day limit, its proration and its order (France
    first) exactly as it does for the record.
NOT COVERED, AND SAID ON THE SCREEN ALWAYS: telework from a country
other than France; a second employer; a change of residence during the
year. A case outside the model never produces a confident number.

2. THE SCREEN
====================================================================
- As designed: the scenario choices, the current projection beside the
  simulated one, the rail, the zone badge, the remaining-day or
  over-threshold tiles (R73: the label names the threshold, the figure

  docs/regulatory-sources.md (R146). The fiscal one - beyond 40 percent

  REG-UPDATE. For the social threshold, write a sentence only if a row
  says it; otherwise nothing.
- THE BUTTONS: "Revenir a ma prevision" always. "Adopter ce rythme"
  ONLY for the extra-weekday scenario, where adopting means changing the
  rhythm (still a projection, still nothing recorded). For the rate
  scenario, the action is a link to change the rate in Parametres,
  through R290's confirmation. Missions and extra days have no "adopt" -
  they are recorded as days when they happen.
- Neutral wording: the app shows the consequence and never advises.
