====================================================================
WHAT THE REMINDER MUST NOT DO
====================================================================
State these before building. Each is a way a notification is correct
and unwelcome:

  EXACTLY ONE PER DAY, and only when today is unconfirmed. Not one
  per device, not one per entry, not a second if the first went
  unread. SPEC 13.3: a compliance tool that nags gets uninstalled.
  NO SECOND REMINDER, no weekly digest, no streak.
  IT IS OPT-IN. reminderEnabled is false until a person turns it on,
  and turning it off takes effect on the next run with no persuasion.
  IT CARRIES THE PROPOSAL in auto mode - the day detected, with
  confirm and correct actions - because a notification that only
  says "open the app" has spent a person's attention to tell them
  nothing.
  IT IS IDEMPOTENT ON (user, date). A scheduler that retries must not
  deliver twice, and proving that is this task's central test.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
====================================================================
AC1. pg-boss starts ONCE - in instrumentation.ts behind a globalThis
     guard - and a test proves a second start does not create a second
     scheduler. Dev hot-reload and a two-instance run must not
     double-send.
AC2. One reminder per unconfirmed day, none on a confirmed day, none
     after opt-out, and none twice when the job is retried - all four
     asserted against a fake clock and a fake transport, with R244's
     counts recorded by the job itself.
AC3. The purge removes rows at 31 days and leaves rows at 29,
     asserted at the boundary; dead tokens are pruned on 404 and 410
     and NOT on a transient failure, which is the distinction that
     decides whether a person silently stops receiving reminders.
