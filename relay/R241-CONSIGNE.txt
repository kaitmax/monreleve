R241 - CONTINUOUS EXECUTION
The standing consigne. Paste into any running session, and carry it
verbatim in every prompt from FW27 onward.

====================================================================
RUN THE WHOLE TASK WITHOUT RETURNING TO THE OPERATOR. THE ONLY THING
THAT ENDS THE SESSION IS THE REPORT, COMMITTED AND PUSHED. Nothing
else is a stopping point.

In particular, do NOT stop to:
  - say an increment is done, or that a gate is running;
  - summarise progress, or ask whether to continue;
  - ask a question you could park;
  - report a finding, however good - it goes in the report;
  - wait for acknowledgement of anything.
If you find yourself composing a message that is not the report,
that is the signal to keep working instead.

WHEN SOMETHING IS UNCLEAR: R156. Take the conservative, reversible
option - the one that claims less, ships less, and can be changed
tomorrow without a migration, a 301, or a correction to something
already published - write the PARK entry, and CONTINUE.

THE FOUR THINGS THAT DO END THE SESSION EARLY. Each ends it with the
report written, the tree clean, and everything so far pushed:

  1. R156's boundary.
     - a PERMANENT decision: a slug, a URL parameter name, a
       database column needing a migration, an anchor, a published
       redirect;
     - anything that would publish a FALSE OR UNSOURCED claim -
       R146's three options are exhaustive and (c) "not written" is
       always available, so this should never be what forces a stop;
     - a gate, budget or threshold change;
     - a new RUNTIME dependency;
     - anything touching lib/calc-engine.
  2. A red gate you cannot fix inside the increment.
  3. Any path where a test or a job could reach a real person
     (R243's shape, wherever it applies).
  4. A degraded context: you cannot find a file you know exists, or
     a command's output stops matching what you expect.

A session that stops for one of these is a session that succeeded.
A session that stops to talk is not.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE. Every increment is still gated and pushed on
its own green chain - this removes the conversation BETWEEN
increments, never the discipline WITHIN one. gate:clone still runs
before every push. The walkthrough (R176/R177) and the read-through
still happen, and they are the two instruments that have found what
no gate could, in every task for five tasks running.

AND THE REPORT CARRIES WHAT YOU WOULD HAVE SAID. Every parked
question with the option taken and the one not taken; every defect
found by reading; every plant and whether it landed; every guard that
fired on your own work. A report that says "went well" after a silent
run has told the operator less than a stop would have.

WHY THIS EXISTS, so it is not mistaken for impatience: the operator
reads one report and rules on everything in it at once. Mid-task
messages cost a round trip each and arrive when the operator cannot
act on them. The trade is real and accepted - some decisions will be
taken conservatively and parked, and one may be reversed in the
morning. R156 is what makes that survivable.
