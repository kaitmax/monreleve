====================================================================
WHAT THE DOCUMENT MUST NOT DO
====================================================================
State these before building. Each is a way the PDF looks right and
is wrong:

  IT DOES NOT CLAIM MORE THAN THE RECORD SUPPORTS. No certifie,
  atteste, conforme, valide, approuve. Only declare, enregistre,
  horodate. FW24 asserts the ban in both directions so silence
  cannot pass - keep that property when the words reach a page.

  IT SURVIVES A MONOCHROME PHOTOCOPY. Every status carries its word.
  Two badges both reading "Seuil depasse" tell a photocopied reader
  nothing; each names WHICH seuil.

  IT PRINTS EVERY DATE, including days with no entry and days of
  absence. A document that silently omits dates invites the question
  of what else it omitted.

  IT DOES NOT RECOMPUTE. Every figure comes from the FW24 payload.

  ITS FOOTER IS UNCONDITIONAL (R183). The site's version branches on
  trackerExists; the document's cannot - every entry in an exported
  dossier IS timestamped.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
====================================================================
AC1. The CSV's columns are exact and stable, asserted against a
     header snapshot, UTF-8 with BOM, and it opens in a spreadsheet
     without mangling accents or turning a date into a number.
AC2. The PDF is A4, carries the rail from renderRailSvg (the same
     generator as the web, not a redrawing), prints every annex row
     including gaps and absences with provenance in words, and
     contains none of the five banned words - asserted by extracting
     text from the produced file, not from the template.
AC3. The print route is token-gated: unauthenticated returns 401,
     another user's year 403, and the token is single-use or
     short-lived - state which and why.
