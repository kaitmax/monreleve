THE OPERATOR'S DECISION, AND WHAT IT CHANGES. Automatic detection
    ships now, opt-in, labelled beta, and tells people plainly when it
    is not sure. The field test still runs; the beta label comes off
    only when it passes. Shipping before validating is acceptable on
    one condition, which every ruling below serves: THE APP NEVER
    CLAIMS AN ACCURACY IT HAS NOT MEASURED.

THE RISK NO MESSAGE CAN COVER, stated so it is never forgotten. A
    "not sure" message helps when the app KNOWS it is unsure. The
    danger is when it is sure and wrong - GPS reflecting off buildings
    in central Geneva, placing someone on the wrong side, rated HIGH.
    The person confirms, telework is understated (R278), and they can
    cross 40 percent without knowing. Only the field test measures how
    often that happens. Everything below keeps that rate as low as it
    can be while it is still unknown.

====================================================================
THE RULINGS
====================================================================
R311 (new). ONLY HIGH PROPOSES. MEDIUM and LOW both withhold and show
    the not-sure message. Fewer proposals, fewer wrong ones. The field
    protocol's measurement still scores HIGH and MEDIUM separately -
    the measurement build is unchanged; only what the PRODUCT shows
    changes. Record this in docs/detection-model.md.

R312 (new). THE NOT-SURE MESSAGE, IN WORDS. When a day withholds, the
    person sees why and what to do, from fr/: something to the effect
    of "Nous ne sommes pas certains de votre position ce jour-la.
    Indiquez-le vous-meme." - final wording yours, reachable (R238),
    never a percentage (R27), never alarming.

R313 (new). THE APP RECOGNISES SOMEONE WHO LIVES NEAR THE BORDER, FROM
    DATA IT ALREADY KEEPS. The daily tally counts how many positions
    fell in the 150 m border strip. When that share stays high, the
    person is told ONCE, in words, that detection is not reliable where
    they live and that recording days manually will be more accurate -
    and given the choice. Their choice is respected either way: manual
    is a peer, never a punishment, and automatic is never forced off
    (D19). No coordinate is needed; the tally is enough.
    THE RULE: a working day counts as near-border when its buffer-fix
    share is above the field protocol's MEDIUM ceiling - above it a day
    cannot even reach MEDIUM. The person is told when that holds on at
    least 3 of their last 5 working days with data. Encode it as named
    constants citing the protocol, mark it PROPOSED in
    docs/detection-model.md, and FREEZE it when the field test starts
    (R276) - a threshold chosen after seeing data is tuned to pass.

R314 (new). A PROPOSAL IS PHRASED AS A PROPOSAL. "Proposition -
    verifiez", with confirm and correct actions; never "vous etiez en
    Suisse". The confirmed day carries provenance DETECTED_CONFIRMED,
    or DETECTED_CORRECTED with detectedKind if changed (R202) - and the
    releve and PDF already show that in words, so an accountant can see
    which days came from detection. Verify they do.

R315 (new). OPT-IN, LABELLED BETA, MANUAL BY DEFAULT. Parametres gains
    "Detection automatique (beta)". R282 said the manual product never
    mentions a feature it lacks; the feature now exists, so the choice
    appears - and nowhere does the app suggest manual is the lesser
    option. Turning it off stops sampling at once and is never a
    confirmation dialog away (SPEC 13). Consent is timestamped both
    ways through /api/v1/me (FW26).

R316 (new, reverses R304). THE LOCATION PERMISSION RETURNS TO THE
    RELEASE BUILD, with the prominent disclosure screen D5 requires,
    shown BEFORE the system prompt. checkReleaseNoLocationPermission
    becomes a check that the release declares EXACTLY the location
    permissions automatic mode needs and nothing more. If the person
    revokes the permission in system settings, the app notices, stops,
    and says so - it falls back to manual, it does not crash or nag.

R317 (new). DO NOT ASK FOR AN EXEMPTION FROM BATTERY OPTIMISATION.
    The permission that requests it is itself restricted on Play. If
    Samsung's battery management stops background sampling - and on
    this S9 it may - say so in words in the help text, link to nothing
    that changes system settings on the person's behalf, and record
    what you observed.
