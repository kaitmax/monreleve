====================================================================
WHY THE SEED WRITES TO THE DATABASE, NOT THROUGH THE API
====================================================================
The server stamps firstRecordedAt itself, at the moment it receives an
entry - that is the product's evidence model. A year sent through the
API today would be stamped today, so EVERY day would be marked "saisie
tardive". A realistic year needs entries whose server timestamps fall
when a real person would have recorded them: most on the day itself,
some a few days later, a few genuinely late. Only a direct seed into
the local database can produce that, with an EntryEvent trail that
matches each entry (the append-only triggers refuse updates and
deletes, not inserts). Every timestamp is in the past - never after
the moment the seed runs. A record stamped in the future is a world
that cannot happen (R197).

====================================================================
WHICH YEARS
====================================================================
- The RuleSet covers 2026 only, and it is part of the frozen engine. A
  COMPLETE twelve-month year has to be a past year, so:
    2025, JANUARY TO DECEMBER - the full year. Its releve will carry
      RULE_SET_FALLBACK, because 2025's rules are not encoded. That
      warning is honest and stays. (The 40 percent rule with up to 10
      mission days applied in 2025 under the transitional agreement and
      continues under the 2026 amendment, but the app may only say so
      once a 2025 RuleSet and its source row exist - not this task.)
    2026, 1 JANUARY TO YESTERDAY - the current, provisional year, as a
      real person sees it today.
  A person in their second year has exactly this shape.
- NO ENTRY AFTER YESTERDAY. Future days are refused by the product, and
  a future-dated record is an impossible world.
