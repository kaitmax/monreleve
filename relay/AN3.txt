CONTEXT
====================================================================
AN3 ONLY: the shared visual components - the rail, the badge, the
rate display, the metric tile, the day cell, the legend, the
confidence tier - and the formatters they depend on.

WHY THIS TASK IS WHERE THE INSTRUMENT STOPS BEING A SCAFFOLD. AN1
built an emulator harness and proved it on a placeholder. From here
every component is a thing a person judges by looking, and six tasks
of web history say the same sentence: a person looking at a rendered
thing has found what no gate could, every time. A baseline certifies
that nothing changed; only a person opening a PNG says whether what
is there is right.

THE COMPONENTS ARE NOT NEW DESIGN. They exist, specified, in
DESIGN-SYSTEM.md and in the FD briefs, and the web implementations
are the reference for behaviour. The risk is not invention, it is
DRIFT: a rail whose ticks scale, a badge that reads its colour from a
Status instead of a Zone, a tile that renders a minus sign, a
formatter that loses a no-break space.

Read: DESIGN-SYSTEM.md sections 1, 2, 3 and 4 (the token block is
normative); SPEC 6 (zones, statuses, the rail, French formatting),
SPEC 11 (where each component lands), SPEC 20;
web/lib/ui/core/** and web/lib/ui/react/** as the behavioural
reference. If prompts/design-frontier/FD3, FD4, FD6, FD7 are absent,
SAY SO (R231) and build from SPEC and DESIGN-SYSTEM, which are the
named authority.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
====================================================================
AC1. THE FORMATTERS ARE CODEPOINT-EXACT AND MATCH THE WEB'S BYTES:
     formatPercent(34.6) is "34,6" + U+00A0 + "%", formatDays(1234.5)
     groups with U+202F, formatDate is Swiss dots, formatDateTime
     puts U+00A0 on BOTH sides of the a-grave (R29). Asserted by
     codepoint, never by visual equality, and R26's exhaustive
     round-trips ported: for every integer n in 0..10000,
     formatPercent(n/100) renders the digits of n at two decimals.
AC2. THE RAIL MATCHES THE WEB'S OUTPUT, and the comparison is made:
     same RailData, the web's renderRailSvg geometry versus the
     Compose rendering - fill fraction, tick positions at 40 and
     49,9, tick width 2 dp NOT SCALED (R130), labels anchored
     outward (R39), band names absent at narrow widths (R45), the
     three static bands only in the social variant (R48). Where an
     exact pixel comparison is not possible across the two
     technologies, compare the GEOMETRY NUMBERS and say so.
AC3. EVERY COMPONENT IN EVERY STATE IS CAPTURED ON A DEVICE AND
     LOOKED AT. Not a Paparazzi baseline - those certify stability.
     The instrument from AN1: emulator, screencap, open the PNG,
     describe what is on it. The report says what was seen, per
     component, in words. This is the acceptance criterion the whole
     task turns on.

INCREMENT 1 - the formatters (AC1)
====================================================================
- fr/format/: percent, days, signed days, date, date-time. Inter's
  tabular numerals are a rendering concern, not a formatting one -
  keep them separate.
- The web's are the reference. R206: do not transcribe the French
  strings; the copy lives in fr/ and the formatter takes values.
- R26's exhaustive loops, ported. 10 001 and 1 001 cases,
  deterministic, not property tests.
- Kotlin traps to prove against, each with a test: String.format is
  locale-sensitive and will emit a period in some locales; DecimalFormat
  carries a DecimalFormatSymbols that must be pinned, not defaulted;
  and the JVM's default locale on a device is the USER's, which is
  exactly the thing that must not leak into a French document.
- Commit: "AN3: the formatters, codepoint-exact". Push.

INCREMENT 3 - the rail (AC2)
====================================================================
- The rail as a Compose component: 8 dp pill track, fill in the band
  colour, two ticks at 40 and 49,9, 2 dp wide, NOT SCALING with the
  rail's width (R130 - vector-effect's equivalent; if Compose has no
  direct analogue, compute in dp from the density and say so).
- Labels anchored outward (R39): 40 % ends at its tick, 49,9 %
  starts at its tick. They can then never collide, at any width.
- Band names absent in inline and compact (R45); the React wrapper
  renders them as a legend, so the Compose equivalent is a separate
  legend composable, not text inside the rail.
- The social variant (R48): three static bands, no fill by rate, no
  rate at all (R44). It is generic by construction.
- AC2's geometry comparison against the web's numbers.
- A non-finite rate renders the unavailable state and NEVER calls
  zoneFor (R33). Assert zoneFor is not reached.
- Commit: "AN3: the threshold rail". Push.

====================================================================
INCREMENT 4 - badge, rate, tile, day cell, legend, confidence
====================================================================
- ZoneBadge: pill chip, soft fill, icon, label, explanation beneath.
  Warning label colour is text-primary on the soft fill, NOT the
  warning colour (R57) - that pairing measured 3,42:1 on the web and
  fails AA at body size.
- RateDisplay: Display size, tabular, band-coloured, with the
  secondary rawRate block and its explanatory line when the two
  differ (R224). The warning-band hero at Display size is the ONE
  permitted large-text use of the warning colour, and the contrast
  test applies the large-text threshold explicitly rather than
  exempting it.
- MetricTile: label, figure, and the negative variant where the
  label NAMES THE THRESHOLD from the RuleSet (R73) and the figure is
  the absolute value - never a minus sign, never clamped to zero.
- DayCell: date, the kind letter with its country dot (S/D/M/A), the
  4 dp quality bar (green on time, amber late, grey missing, none
  for weekend or future), the corner mark for a corrected detection.
  Colour means RECORD QUALITY; the letter and dot mean WHERE. A test
  asserts a legend exists wherever a DayCell grid does - letters
  need a key (R119's shape).
- ConfidenceTier: words plus icon plus three segments, NEVER a
  percentage (R27).
- Commit: "AN3: badge, rate, tile, day cell, legend, confidence".
  Push.
