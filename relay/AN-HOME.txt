1. THE CHOICE: CLOSE THE OPTIONS, SHOW THE ANSWER, OFFER "MODIFIER"
====================================================================
The operator's main request. Today the choices stay on screen after a
day is recorded. Instead:

  BEFORE A CHOICE - the options as a VERTICAL list of Material 3 list
  items in one white card with a 1 px border and no shadow. Vertical,
  not side by side: four or five options in a row cannot fit at 200
  percent text (R271). Every kind the product records stays: En Suisse,
  Domicile, Mission en France, Mission dans un pays tiers, and Absent
  with its reason - Absent may sit behind a secondary row, but it does
  not disappear.

  AFTER THE TAP - the options close and are replaced, in the same card,
  by one compact line with the answer and a "Modifier" TEXT button
  (a word, not a pencil alone - easier to find, and TalkBack reads it):
    - with half-days, the line shows BOTH halves: "Matin : Domicile -
      Apres-midi : En Suisse" - a single label would misstate a split
      day (R292);
    - the line tells the truth about syncing, as it does today:
      "enregistre sur le telephone, en attente d'envoi" until the server
      has it, then "enregistre" - persistent, never a toast;
    - a day confirmed from a detection proposal says so, as it does
      today ("propose par la detection, confirme par vous").

  "MODIFIER" reopens the options with the current choice marked.
  Choosing records exactly as today. An "Annuler" closes them again
  WITHOUT writing anything.

  THE MOTION - DESIGN-SYSTEM's tokens, not invented numbers: the
  options fade out and the card's height shrinks to the answer line
  over the 200 ms standard duration, no bounce. Reopening is the
  reverse. Number changes in the year panel animate over 300 ms, as the
  design system says. If motion tokens do not exist in Kotlin yet, add
  them citing DESIGN-SYSTEM (R77) - never a duration typed inline.
  NO CELEBRATION: no checkmark burst, no scaling, no confetti, no
  "bravo" - DESIGN-SYSTEM forbids a success animation on logging. The
  confirmation IS the answer appearing.

  ACCESSIBILITY OF THE CHANGE: when the options close, TalkBack
  announces the answer (a polite live region), and focus lands on the
  answer line or its "Modifier" - never lost. With Android's "Remove
  animations" setting on, the change is instant and nothing is left
  half-drawn.

  STATE (R310): whether the options are open for editing survives
  rotation, a text-size change, dark mode and a background kill, like
  every other state on the screen.

already holds and components that already exist; nothing here is a new

  A. THE HEADER - a greeting and today's date in Europe/Paris, worked

  B. TODAY - the choice card from section 1. In automatic mode, its

     choices), and the near-border notice once. WORDING STAYS AS BUILT.

  C. TO ATTEND TO - shown ONLY when there is something, never as an

     calendar; days still waiting to be sent; and R286's warning as the

     first. When there is nothing, the section is absent.

  D. THE YEAR - the existing ZoneBadge, RateDisplay with the raw rate
     when it differs (R224), the ThresholdRail, and the two remaining-
     day tiles, negative named by threshold when over (R73). This is
     the product's core answer; give it the visual weight of a hero
     card.

  E. THIS WEEK - Monday to Friday as five of the existing DayCells,
     with the legend a DayCell grid always carries (R119). Tapping a day

     calendar's rule and becomes a list (R271).

  scores; a map or live position (D18, DESIGN-SYSTEM section 2); any
