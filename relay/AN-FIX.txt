R303 (new, and it is the point of this task). THE DATA ON THE PHONE
    BELONGS TO ONE ACCOUNT, RECORDED FROM THE MOMENT OF THE TAP.
    F4: account A recorded two days offline, the session expired,
    account B signed in, and A's days synced under B's token into B's
    record. Every pending mutation is tagged with the account that
    recorded it, AT THE TAP, the way its mutation id is. Then:
      - the sync worker sends ONLY the current account's rows;
      - the calendar, Aujourd'hui and the releve show ONLY the current
        account's rows - an expired session is not a sign-out, so
        R295's clearing never ran and B may otherwise SEE A's days;
      - if a different account signs in while another account's days
        are pending, those days are SET ASIDE: not sent, not shown,
        kept until their account signs back in, and then sent under
        that account.
    Setting aside rather than deleting is R156's conservative option:
    nothing is lost and it can be undone. Deleting would destroy A's
    days without A ever deciding. R295's sign-out still clears
    everything, set-aside rows included.

R304 (new). NO LOCATION PERMISSION IN THE RELEASE BUILD UNTIL
    DETECTION SHIPS. F2 and F6: the manual product declares
    ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and never uses it. That is the item most
    likely to draw Play review, and it breaks R282: asking for a
    permission claims the feature. Detection cannot ship before AN6b's
    field test. The release manifest declares no location permission;
    the permission moves to the build AN6b's testers install. The
    detection code stays in the repository.
