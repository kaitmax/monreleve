CONTEXT
====================================================================
FW24 ONLY: the dossier service. GET /api/v1/dossier?year= returns the
computed statement the Android Releve screen renders and the PDF
prints. This is where the engine's output becomes the document.

Read: SPEC 12 (the dossier, verbatim), SPEC 7 (the engine's API),
SPEC 8, SPEC 20; fixtures/geneva-cases.json; REPORT-FW23.txt.

====================================================================
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
====================================================================
AC1. For the seeded year, the metrics and EVERY monthly cumulative
     percentage match a direct calculateTelework call exactly,
     including truncation - 12,91 % and not 12,92 %. Asserted against
     codepoint literals (R96).
AC2. ABSENT rows never reach the engine: engine output is identical
     for the seeded year with and without them, and absences are
     counted once in the identification block as "Absences
     declarees".
AC3. The annex includes GAP ROWS - a working day with no entry is
     returned as "Non renseigne", never omitted - and every entry
     carries its provenance in words, its first timestamp, its
     last-modified when different, its clientClaimedAt when synced
     offline, and its late flag.

====================================================================
INCREMENTS
====================================================================
1. Aggregation: entries for a year -> TeleworkInput. HOME is
   telework; MISSION_FR and MISSION_THIRD stay separate; FULL is 1
   and AM/PM are 0,5; granularity and denominator from User;
   periodFraction from activityStartDate; taxYear from the requested
   year, and RULE_SET_FALLBACK surfaces when it differs (R11).
   ABSENT filtered before the engine sees anything. RED first.
   Commit: "FW24: aggregation to TeleworkInput".
2. The monthly breakdown: Suisse / Domicile / Mission FR / Mission
   tiers / cumulative declarable percentage, chronological.
   R120: the fixture set must include a month where two derivations
   of the same figure disagree, so a test cannot pass by using the
   wrong one - the FW12 tile defect is the precedent.
   Commit: "FW24: the monthly breakdown".
3. The evidence annex: every entry plus gap rows, with provenance
   rendered as a French phrase rather than an enum leaked to the
   client. Late flag derived from firstRecordedAt - dayDate > 7 days.
   Commit: "FW24: the evidence annex".
4. The payload: identification block (titulaire, canton, taux,
   methode, mode d'enregistrement, periode, absences declarees,
   generated at), four metrics, the engine result, the monthly table,
   the annex, and the provisional flag when the year is not closed.
   A year with no entries returns an honest empty payload, not zeros.
   Commit: "FW24: the dossier payload".
5. docs/api-v1.md extended; the drift test still passes.
   Commit: "FW24: contract document".
6. Walkthrough (R176): fetch the dossier for the seeded year and read
   it. Does it describe a year a person lived? Check one month by
   hand against the seed. Report what you checked.
   Commit: "FW24: walkthrough and report".
