CONTEXT
====================================================================
FW24 ONLY: the dossier service. GET /api/v1/dossier?year= returns the
computed Releve annuel de teletravail as data. FW25 renders it; the
Android app displays it; the PDF is printed from it.

THIS IS WHERE THE ENGINE MEETS REAL ROWS FOR THE FIRST TIME. Until
now calculateTelework has been fed fixtures written by hand. FW24
feeds it aggregated database rows, and the aggregation is new surface
sitting between two verified things.

Read: SPEC 12 (the dossier, verbatim), SPEC 7 (the engine's API and

docs/regulatory-sources.md; docs/api-v1.md as FW23 froze it;
fixtures/geneva-cases.json.

====================================================================
WHAT THE SERVICE MUST NOT DO
====================================================================
State these before building, because each is a way the dossier could
look right and be wrong:

  IT DOES NOT RECOMPUTE ANYTHING THE ENGINE COMPUTES. The rate, the
  headroom, the credited missions, the status - all come from
  calculateTelework. The service aggregates rows into a
  TeleworkInput and formats a TeleworkResult. FW12's tile defect was
  a panel recomputing a value the engine had already given it, and
  it passed because every fixture agreed.

  ABSENCES NEVER REACH THE ENGINE. Filter them before building the
  input, and assert that a year with and without absence rows yields
  identical engine output (SPEC 8, the absence rule).

  IT DOES NOT TYPE A THRESHOLD. Every figure comes from the year's
  RuleSet (R147), including in any prose the payload carries.

  IT DOES NOT SKIP A DAY. Unrecorded working days appear as
  Non renseigne rows. A document that silently omits dates invites
  the question of what else it omitted.

  IT DOES NOT CLAIM A LEGAL REVIEW. Nothing in the payload asserts
  verification beyond what docs/regulatory-sources.md supports.

====================================================================
ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
====================================================================
AC1. For the demo year, every figure in the payload matches a DIRECT
     calculateTelework call on the same aggregated input - asserted
     field by field, not by snapshot (SPEC 7.6 forbids snapshots in
     the engine and the same reasoning applies to its consumer).
     The monthly cumulative percentages truncate: 12,91 % not 12,92 %.
AC2. The evidence annex carries every entry with its date, portion,
     kind, note, provenance IN WORDS, firstRecordedAt, lastModifiedAt
     when different, clientClaimedAt when present, and the late flag -
     plus Non renseigne rows for unrecorded working days and absence
     rows counted only in the identification block.
AC3. A year with no entries returns an honest empty payload, not
     zeros; a partial year is marked provisional with the period
     covered.
