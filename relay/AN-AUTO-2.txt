R320 (new, the operator's decision, and it comes FIRST). DETECTION
    CHECKS THE POSITION FOUR TIMES A DAY, NOT ALL DAY. Continuous sampling
    was never fully realistic - Android 8 and later deliver background
    location only a few times an hour - and four checks cost a fraction
    of the battery. It also makes the disclosure plainer and more
    honest: the person is told exactly when their position is checked.
      THE FOUR WINDOWS, in Europe/Paris time, chosen to avoid lunch -
      the least reliable moment of a working day, when people go out:
          09:30   11:00   14:30   16:30
      Each check runs anywhere INSIDE a window around its time (state
      the window length you choose and why). Android cannot promise an
      exact time in the background, and the permission for exact alarms
      is restricted to alarm and calendar apps - DO NOT REQUEST IT. A
      check that cannot run inside its window is recorded as MISSED, never
      taken late: an 11:00 check delayed until 13:00 would sample lunch,
      which is the very thing the windows avoid.
      HOW A DAY IS DECIDED - a vote, replacing the minutes-and-hysteresis
      tally, which assumed continuous sampling:
          HIGH    at least 3 checks taken, ALL on the same side, none in
                  the 150 m border strip;
          MEDIUM  at least 3 taken, exactly one on the other side or in
                  the strip;
          LOW     anything else.
      Under R311 only HIGH proposes; MEDIUM and LOW show the not-sure
      message. With half-day granularity, each half is decided from its
      own two checks: both taken, both on the same side, neither in the
      strip, or that half is not sure. One bad GPS reading can never
      decide a day or a half-day on its own.
      R313, TRANSLATED: a day counts as near-border when 2 or more of its
      4 checks land in the strip - above the protocol's MEDIUM ceiling of
      40 percent. The person is told on 3 of their last 5 working days
      with data, as before.
      THESE THRESHOLDS ARE PROPOSED. Record them in
      docs/detection-model.md and rewrite docs/field-protocol-AN6b.md
      section 3 in terms of checks rather than minutes. The operator
      ratifies them; they FREEZE when the field test starts (R276).
      THE FIELD TEST MUST TEST WHAT SHIPS. The measurement build changes
      to the same four-window sampling and the same vote. A field test of
      continuous sampling would validate a detector nobody uses.
