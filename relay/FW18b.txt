SOURCE. CLEISS - Centre des Liaisons Europeennes et Internationales de
Securite Sociale, a French public body. An authority, not an advisory
site.
  https://www.cleiss.fr/faq/droit_d_option_des_frontaliers_france-suisse.html
  CONFIRM THIS URL WITH THE OPERATOR AT INCREMENT 0 before writing the
  row. It is the page a search located and its content matches the pass
  on every point, but the row must cite what the operator actually read.

THE WINDOW. Three months. CONFIRMED.

THE TRIGGER, and this is the part everyone gets wrong. The three months
run from ANY of four events:
  1. taking up work in Switzerland;
  2. resuming work after a period of unemployment;
  3. taking up residence in France;
  4. moving from worker to pensioner status.
Every advisor writes "three months from hiring", which is WRONG for a
frontalier who was already working in Switzerland and then moves to
France. This is the guide's most valuable paragraph.

IRREVOCABILITY. The right may be exercised ONCE and it is irrevocable.

WHAT REOPENS IT. Only a change of situation: resuming work in
Switzerland, transferring residence to France, or retiring on a Swiss
pension alone.

WHAT DOES NOT REOPEN IT, named explicitly by CLEISS: marriage, divorce,
a birth or a death in the family, or changing jobs without an
unemployment gap between them.

THE DEFAULT. Failing to opt within three months means automatic
affiliation in Switzerland.

LEGAL BASIS. The ALCP, in force 1 June 2002; Regulation (CE) 883/2004
applicable between Switzerland and the EU since 1 April 2012; the
modalities from the accord of 7 July 2016.
