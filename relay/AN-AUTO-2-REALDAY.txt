AN-AUTO-2-REALDAY - Mon Releve
THE REAL DAY OF CHECKS AND THE BATTERY, MEASURED THE WAY A PERSON USES
THE PHONE
Two short sessions, each pasted into a fresh Claude Code session at the
repository root: PART A before the first window, PART B after the day
has concluded. The phone (Galaxy S9) is connected for both.

====================================================================
WHY THIS REPLACES THE PLUGGED-IN READ
====================================================================
AN-AUTO-2 armed the phone PLUGGED IN with the screen kept on, and reset
batterystats at that moment. Two problems, both about measuring the
wrong thing (R233 - a measurement reports whether it observed its
subject):

  1. BATTERY. batterystats counts DISCHARGE. A phone on its charger all
     day is not discharging, so a battery figure from that day says
     almost nothing about the 3 percent budget. The figure must come
     from a day OFF the charger.
  2. MISSED CHECKS. Plugged in with the screen on, Android never enters
     Doze, so the background work is not delayed. The report already
     records the miss count as a lower bound. A person's phone is in a
     pocket, screen off, unplugged - which is exactly when Samsung's
     battery management delays background work (R317).

And one thing to check before anything else:

  3. THE DAY OF THE WEEK. The checks were armed on 2026-10-03, a
     SATURDAY. If the sampler only runs on working days - which the
     product's working-day logic suggests - a Saturday produces no
     checks, and the read would find nothing for a reason that has
     nothing to do with detection. Find out from the code.

Nothing here changes code. Same rules as AN-AUTO-2: D18 and D16
absolute, no red commit, restore every phone setting changed, R270, and
advisors for judgment calls. Each part ends with its own short report,
committed and pushed. Any handoff goes in relay/ (R319).

====================================================================
PART A - ARM IT FOR A REAL DAY (run before the first window)
====================================================================
1. THE DAY. From the code, establish whether the sampler and the vote
   run on non-working days. State the answer and the file and line that
   decide it. If it skips weekends and today is a weekend, STOP HERE:
   say which day to use (the next working day) and end with a one-line
   report. Do not change the code to sample on a weekend - that would
   test a behaviour the product does not have.
2. THE WINDOWS FOR THAT DAY. Compute the four check times in the
   phone's local time from the date - Paris summer time ends on the
   last Sunday of October, after which Paris and Casablanca share the
   same clock. Do not take times from an earlier report.
3. ARM. Confirm automatic mode is on, both location permissions are
   granted, the worker is enqueued, and the mock provider is denied.
   Restore stay_on_while_plugged_in to its original value - the phone
   must be free to sleep normally.
4. RESET THE BATTERY BASELINE, LAST: `adb shell dumpsys batterystats
   --reset`, immediately before the operator unplugs. Record the time.
5. END with a message that tells the operator plainly: unplug the phone
   now, use it normally or leave it face-down and idle - screen off,
   not charging - and plug it back in after the last check has
   concluded, at the time you computed. Write that time.
   Commit a short PART A note in relay/AN-AUTO-2-realday-A.txt. Push.

====================================================================
PART B - READ IT (run after the day has concluded)
====================================================================
1. Plug in. Confirm the phone was NOT charging during the day: read the
   unplug and replug times from batterystats. If it charged for part of
   the day, say for how long - the battery figure covers only the
   discharge.
2. Follow relay/AN-AUTO-2-handoff-2.txt's read recipe for the tally:
   each of the four checks - taken inside its window, or missed - and
   the day's outcome under R320's vote. In Casablanca, every taken check
   should be outside Switzerland, so a HIGH "Domicile" is correct; a
   missed check is the finding to record, with its window.
3. THE BATTERY: the app's share of discharge across the unplugged day,
   by AN6a's method, against the 3 percent budget - and R233's line on
   whether the measurement observed the app at all.
4. THE A7 STATE the handoff names: the proposal card's Corriger-open
   state surviving a rotation and a background kill, now that a
   concluded day has a proposal.
5. RESTORE every phone setting the handoff lists. Gates and gate:clone
   if anything was committed beyond the report.
6. REPORT, appended to REPORT-AN-AUTO-2.txt as a dated section: each
   check (taken or missed, with its window), the outcome, the battery
   figure and how long the phone was off the charger, the A7 state, and
   what Samsung's battery management did under real conditions.
   Commit, push.
