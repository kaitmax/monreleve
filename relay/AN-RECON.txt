====================================================================
1. THE COMPARISON
====================================================================
- For a COMPLETED year only - the current year has no employer figure
  yet. The person enters the rate from their attestation, French
  format with a comma, two decimals.
- R342 (new). THE EMPLOYER'S FIGURE STAYS ON THE PHONE, tagged with the
  account and cleared on sign-out (R303, R295) - no new server field.
- IT IS COMPARED WITH THE SERVER'S DOSSIER for that year - the releve's
  figure (R287) - never with a phone-side calculation. If days are still
  unsynced, the screen says so, as the releve does.
- THE GAP in points, and "environ N jours" on the year's basis (240, or
  the exact total) - computed in one tested function, rounded down to
  the half-day, never UI arithmetic.

====================================================================
2. WHERE THE GAP MAY COME FROM - ONLY FACTS FROM THE RECORD
====================================================================
Each line is a fact the app can check in the person's own record:
  - missions recorded, and how many counted against the 10-day limit;
  - the calculation method the record uses;
  - working days left unrecorded that year;
  - and, from the verified row, that with several employers each
    declares the rate for its own days only.
Each opens the relevant days. Never a guess about the employer.
"Votre releve ne dit pas qui a raison" - the app never says who is
right. The vocabulary ban holds.
