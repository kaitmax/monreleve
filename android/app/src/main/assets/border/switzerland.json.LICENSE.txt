switzerland.json — licence and attribution
===========================================

This file (switzerland.json) is a simplified polygon of the Swiss national
boundary, derived from OpenStreetMap data.

  Data source:  OpenStreetMap, relation 51701 (admin_level=2, Switzerland)
  Processing:   Douglas-Peucker simplification at 25 m
                (android/scripts/prepare-border-polygon.mjs)
