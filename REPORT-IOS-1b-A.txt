Android reads them from the shared resources". A HARD BOUNDARY says
Aujourd'hui's "Compose UI tests and accessibility tests pass unedited in
their assertions". They collide on one artefact: the answer line's glyph

(KindIconMappingTest by reflection over R.drawable). Move the XMLs out of
:app's res and those tests do not compile. A hard boundary outranks a
ruling's mechanism, so - R156: conservative, reversible, recorded - the
build keeps the boundary and asks:
  (i)  AS BUILT: the XMLs' one home stays app/src/main/res/drawable; Android
       draws them as today (pixel-identical by construction, no Paparazzi
       seam, no ContentProvider or assets in the APK); the shared screen names
       a glyph by an enum and reaches the painter through a seam; iOS gets
       Compose Multiplatform resources GENERATED from the app's XMLs by a
       Gradle task, and the path-comparison test proves the generator
       delivered (R154). R603's "Android reads from the shared resources" is
       NOT met.
  (ii) R603 LITERALLY: the XMLs to commonMain composeResources, the
       semantics key changes type -> assertion edits in the three tests
       above, plus the Paparazzi context seam (the spike's reflection into
       CMP's internal AndroidContextProvider) in all 14 screenshot classes.
       Breaches the boundary as written; only the operator can relax it.
  (iii) the XMLs to :shared androidMain/res with android.nonTransitiveRClass
