THE VERSION CHOICE, READ RATHER THAN RECALLED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Maven Central's maven-metadata.xml for symbol-processing-gradle-plugin, KSP
releases for Kotlin 2.1.x:

    2.1.0-1.0.28  2.1.0-1.0.29
    2.1.10-1.0.29 2.1.10-1.0.30 2.1.10-1.0.31
    2.1.20-1.0.31 2.1.20-1.0.32 2.1.20-2.0.0 2.1.20-2.0.1
    2.1.21-2.0.1  2.1.21-2.0.2

THE LINE IS THE POINT, not the number. 1.0.x is KSP1-capable; 2.0.x (from
2.1.20-2.0.0) is KSP2-only. The hard boundary forbids changing the KSP MODE and
ksp.useKSP2 is unset, so staying inside 1.0.x is what keeps that true across the
Kotlin move.

KOTLIN 2.1.10 AND NOT NEWER. It is exactly the compiler that produced
room-runtime 2.7.2's commonMain klib (ABI 1.201.0), which is the single reason
this task exists. Matching it is the smallest move that can work; 2.1.20 would
also offer a 1.0.x KSP and moves the compiler further for no stated reason.

KSP1 IS NOT REMOVED AT THIS VERSION. It was deprecated in KSP 2.3.0, whose
notice names Kotlin 2.3.0 and AGP 9.0 as the incompatible toolchains. Neither is
in play, and both are a later decision than this one.
