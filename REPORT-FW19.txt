DATA DECISION (R157)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the report's core evidence, so it records what was actually done
rather than what was concluded.

WHAT WAS FOUND
  - The primary source exists and is current. priminfo.admin.ch (OFSP)
    publishes premium data for the 2026 premium year, as PDF and XLSX,
    and states that "Les donnees de primes sont disponibles comme
    fichiers Excel et CSV sous opendata.swiss".
  - The opendata.swiss dataset is "Primes de l'assurance-maladie",
    published by the OFSP, with current resources plus archives back to
    2016. Its granularity, from its description, is canton, age group and
    insurer.

WHAT COULD NOT BE ESTABLISHED
  - THE LICENCE. The opendata.swiss dataset page returns HTTP 403 in this
    environment, and so does the CKAN API
    (ckan.opendata.swiss/api/3/action/package_show). Neither the licence
    name, nor its terms, nor its redistribution conditions could be read.

  R157 forbids "a scraped page without a licence". A dataset whose terms
  are unknown is exactly that, so option (1) is unavailable FROM HERE.

  That distinction is deliberate and it matters for tomorrow: this is a
  limit of this environment, not a finding about the dataset. The data
  appears to be published openly by a federal office; an operator with
  ordinary browser access may be able to read the licence and complete
  option (1) in minutes. P4 says so.

WHY OPTION (2) DOES NOT RESCUE IT
  Option (2) is: the user enters their own premium, the tool computes
  only the comparison and states plainly that it holds no premium data.
  Two problems, and the second is decisive.

  First, the CMU side would also be an input rather than a constant. The
  prompt anticipates this - "if the rate cannot be sourced, it is an
  input, not a constant" - and docs/regulatory-sources.md has no CMU rate
  or threshold; its eight rows are all telework constants. So the tool
  would hold no figure of its own at all: the reader would supply the
  premium AND the rate, and the tool would supply a multiplication and a
  subtraction.

  Second, and this is the one that decides it: THE PAGE'S REQUIRED
  WARNING CANNOT BE WRITTEN. SPEC 9.6 specifies "a prominent error banner
  stating the choice is IRREVOCABLE and the window is THREE MONTHS", and
  this task's own AC2 requires it "above the fold of the result,
  error-coloured, asserted by count and by position".

  Those two facts have no verified row. FW18 parked the droit-option
  guide (P2) for exactly this, and SPEC 22 lists "3-month irrevocable
  LAMal/CMU option" in its OWN inventory of figures "compiled from
  advisory and news sources", to be verified "before the fixtures are
  frozen". Checkpoint A verified six of eight telework constants and
  never reached it.

  So the choices were: publish the banner and assert an unsourced
  regulatory claim, which R156's boundary forbids outright; publish
  without it, which fails AC2 and SPEC 9.6 and removes the single most
  valuable thing on the page; or publish it hedged, which still leaves a
  tool steering a reader toward an irreversible decision on facts it
  cannot state. All three are worse than not publishing.

WHAT WAS REFUSED
  - A premium figure from memory.
  - An averaged estimate presented as a premium.
  - A placeholder number in shipped copy.
  - A scrape of priminfo's XLSX without established terms.
  None of these was attempted; they are listed because R157 names them
  and the report should say plainly that the line was not approached.
